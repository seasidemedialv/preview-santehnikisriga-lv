Plumber in Lucavsala, Riga

A plumber in Lucavsala is rarely needed. The island belongs to the Salas district, which had only 61 residents in 2021, and much of it is taken up by allotments and recreation areas. The work here is usually seasonal: allotment water pipes, taps and pumps on low, flood-prone ground.

Allotments along the Bieķengrāvis

Lucavsala is part of the Salas district (362.8 ha). The allotments have historically lain along the Bieķengrāvis channel, while the centre and north-west of Lucavsala are zoned as mixed and centre development areas. How the allotments get their water is not described in public sources.

In an allotment cabin the plumbing is usually simple: a seasonal water pipe, a tap, sometimes a sink, a shower or a small water heater (boiler). For a system like this the biggest risk is not wear but frost and the water left in the pipes after the season.

A low island with a clay cover

In the Salas district the groundwater is high, only in a few places deeper than 1.5 m, and there are lenses of silt. In the north of Lucavsala and along its southern and south-western shore the flood risk is up to 10%, in the centre up to 2%. The island is covered by a layer of sandy loam and clay 0.5–1.5 m thick, and the ground lies 2–3 m above sea level, on the southern shore down to 1 m.

  • Clay absorbs water slowly, so after rain the water stands on the surface for a long time and seeps into shallow-laid pipes and pits.
  • A pump that has been under water after a flood must be dried and checked before it is switched on; sump pump repair starts from €75.
  • Water left in pipes and taps in autumn freezes in winter and splits them; thawing frozen pipes costs from €55.
Ground and flood risk: apkaimes.lv, geography of Salas; clay cover and relief: Wikipedia. Checked 05.11.2026.

If the allotment has a shower or a water heater

A small electric water heater in a garden cabin is the most vulnerable appliance in winter: if water is left in it, frost can split the tank or the safety valve. In autumn the water heater must be unplugged, the supply shut off and the water heater drained through the drain cock or by disconnecting the supply pipe.

In spring, before switching it on, the water heater must first be filled with water, and only then should the power be switched on. If it leaks or does not heat, water heater repair starts from €45.

Rainwater outfalls by the Daugava

In 2023 two rainwater sewer outfall pipes were built on Lucavsala by the Daugava, following a 2017 design. They carry rainwater into the river, not household waste water, so allotment drains must not be connected to them.
