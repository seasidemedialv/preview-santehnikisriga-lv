How a plumber's price is made up

The table shows starting prices for the simplest version of each job. The final price is made up of labour and materials, and we give it once we know the situation.

  • Call-out: an emergency call-out starts from €35. For pump work the starting price is a diagnostic visit that includes a simple repair.
  • Labour: the price depends on access (a boxed-in pipe, tiles, a narrow niche), the condition of old parts (rusted threads, a cemented-in toilet, cast iron pipes) and how many jobs are done in one visit.
  • Materials: parts, pipes, traps and appliances are charged separately unless the page says otherwise. You can also buy the appliance yourself.
  • Timing: we also work evenings, nights and weekends when a plumber is free. Even then we agree the price before the work starts.

To get a more accurate price in the first conversation, describe the situation in the form. If you are not sure which job you need, start with the plumbing services page or simply describe the problem.

Three examples of how the price is calculated

  1. 1

    Replacing an old toilet with a new one

    Replacing a floor-standing toilet costs from €60 for labour. On top of that comes the toilet with its cistern and, if the old flexible connector or supply hose does not fit, new parts. If the toilet is intact and only the cistern leaks, a repair from €30 is enough.

  2. 2

    Bath replacement with removal of the old bath

    Removal from €30 plus installing the new bath from €80 gives a labour total from €110. If a dripping mixer tap has to be fixed in the same visit, add from €25, making the total from €135. The bath, the trap and taking away the old bath are not included.

  3. 3

    Replacing three radiators in an apartment

    Radiator replacement costs from €70 each, so labour for three radiators starts from €210. On top of that come the radiators, valves and brackets, plus the house manager's fee for draining and refilling the riser according to their price list.

What is not included in the plumbing price

  • Materials, parts and appliances: toilet, bath, water heater, radiator, mixer tap, pipes and fittings.
  • House manager services: shutting off, draining, refilling and bleeding the riser. The house manager does these according to their own price list.
  • Design documents and calculations, if the house manager requires them, for example for a radiator replacement.
  • Finishing work: tiling, restoring boxing and walls, wall chasing.
  • Removal and disposal of old fixtures, unless agreed.

The article who pays for riser replacement explains why part of the cost of riser work is the building's responsibility. If you are looking for a plumber in general, start on the home page: plumber in Riga.

How much does a plumber cost per hour

People often ask how much a plumber costs per hour. Our prices are per job, not per hour: the table shows the starting price of each job. That way you know the cost before the work, instead of counting minutes while the plumber looks for the leak.

An hourly rate looks simple, but it does not tell you the final cost. The same mixer tap can take half an hour or two hours if the nuts are rusted or the shut-off valve does not close. A per-job price already includes this risk in the agreed amount.

The simplest plumbing jobs start from €25, an emergency call-out from €35. If a job is longer and it is not clear how much it will involve, for example tracing pipes inside a wall, we tell you in advance and agree the price before the work. Describe the situation in the form, and the first estimate will be more accurate.

A plumbing estimate for larger jobs

For a bathroom renovation, replacing the pipes in an apartment or plumbing rough-in, one line in a table is not enough. In that case we prepare a plumbing estimate with each job listed separately.

  • Work item by item: removal, pipework, pressure test, installation of each fixture.
  • Materials: pipes, fittings, valves, traps. If you buy the fixtures yourself, they are not included in the estimate.
  • What is not included: tiling, wall chasing and finishing, the house manager's fee for shutting off the riser.
  • Order of work: what happens before tiling and what happens after it.

When you compare several estimates, check that they contain the same work. A cheaper estimate often simply leaves out the removal or the pressure test. Bathroom plumbing starts from €200, pipe replacement in an apartment from €500 for the work.
