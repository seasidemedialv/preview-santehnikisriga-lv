Plumber in Mežaparks, Riga

Our plumber in Mežaparks works in one of Riga's oldest areas of detached houses, where many buildings are more than a hundred years old. A new bathroom here often has to coexist with old cast-iron pipes, and the work has to be planned so as not to damage walls and finishes that cannot be made again. Below we describe what makes plumbing work different in Mežaparks.

Villas since 1901 and interwar functionalism

The Mežaparks residential area, about 1.3 km², has been developed since 1901. It has early 20th-century Art Nouveau villas and interwar functionalist houses; more than 250 houses were built in the interwar years alone. The area is a town-planning monument of national importance.

For a plumber this means two things. First, the pipes in the walls and between floors are often several generations old, and there are no drawings of where they run. Second, opening up walls or making a new outlet hole in the facade is not a minor decision, so before such work find out what approvals the building needs.

In practice we first look for a way to run the new pipes through existing shafts, the basement or built-in cupboards, and only then decide about opening walls.

Sewerage since 1912

The Mežaparks sewerage system with a biological treatment plant was designed in 1912 by Riga Polytechnic Institute professor Arnolds Jenšs and built over the following two years. Later, in the project "Water Management Development in Riga, Phases 1–2" (1996–2006), Rīgas ūdens renewed and extended the networks in Mežaparks.

So the street networks are not a century old, but the house's internal drainage and the connection to the street often are. If a villa still has its original cast-iron pipes, they become furred up with deposits on the inside, and the drainage gradually slows until at some point it stops altogether.

History of the sewerage: lv.wikipedia.org, Mežaparks (Riga). Network renewal: Rīgas ūdens, EU co-funded projects (phases 1–2). Checked 05.11.2026.

How long old pipes last

The summary of Cabinet Regulation No. 906 sets a service life of 60 years for sewer pipelines with cast-iron fittings, and 15–30 years for cold water pipes and other sewer pipes, depending on the type. In an interwar house with its original cast-iron drains, even the longest of these periods has already passed.

That does not mean everything has to be replaced at once. Often it is enough to replace the damaged sections and transitions if the rest of the pipe is still sound. To find out, we first need to see where the pipe runs and what state the joints are in. When buying or selling a villa, a pre-sale plumbing inspection helps to establish this, from €60.

Service lives: Annex 2 to Cabinet Regulation No. 906 (LV portāls summary, 2019). Checked 05.11.2026.

A new bathroom in an old villa

Many villas originally had a single bathroom, and owners now want a second one upstairs or next to a bedroom. The biggest constraint is not the water supply but the drainage: the new bathroom needs a route to the existing soil stack with enough fall, without cutting into the beams.

  • The transition from a new plastic pipe to old cast iron must be made with a purpose-made seal, not with sealant.
  • If the stack has no vent through the roof, a new toilet or bath can siphon the water out of other fittings' traps, and a smell appears in the house.
  • A long hot water run from the water heater (boiler) to a distant bathroom means a long wait; sometimes a second water heater closer by is the better option.

A long inlet through the garden: where the responsibility of Rīgas ūdens ends

Rīgas ūdens provides the service up to the ownership boundary, most often the outer face of the wall of the billing meter chamber, or the street's red line if there is no chamber. Beyond it the owner maintains the system and repairs leaks at their own expense.

On the large plots in Mežaparks, the inlet from the boundary to the house can be long. Signs that it is leaking: a wet patch in the lawn in dry weather, a drop in pressure throughout the house, or water appearing in the basement by the inlet. Water leak repair costs from €45; if the leak can be located without digging along the whole length, the job is smaller.

Ownership boundary: Rīgas ūdens, responsibility boundaries for water services. Checked 05.11.2026.

The dune massif and the shore of Ķīšezers

Most of Mežaparks lies on a dune massif. In the centre the groundwater is deeper than 3 m, elsewhere 1.5–3 m. Conditions are difficult only in a narrow strip along the shore of Ķīšezers (50–100 m) and around Ķīšezera Street.

That is why in most villas the basements are dry, and water there usually means a leaking pipe, not groundwater. Houses by Ķīšezers are different: there it is worth keeping a sump pump (repair from €75).
