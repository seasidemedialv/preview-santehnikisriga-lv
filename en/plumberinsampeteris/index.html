Plumber in Šampēteris, Riga

A plumber in Šampēteris works in mixed housing: detached houses with gardens, 1990s terraced houses and standard multi-storey blocks at the junction of Irlavas, Stendes and Zalves streets. The city water mains and sewer in Šampēteris were extended back in 2006–2009, so the work here more often involves existing connections than new ones. Below is what matters here.

Phase 3 of the water project: networks in the streets for years

The Šampēteris networks have their own history. In the third phase of the Rīgas ūdens EU-funded project (2006–2009), one of the tasks was extending the water mains and sewer in Šampēteris and Dārzciems, and the name Šampēteris even appears in the title of that component.

If your house was connected at that time, the external supply pipe and the house inlet are now 15–20 years old. At this age the first problems usually appear not in the pipe itself but in the joints, the stop valves and where the inlet passes through the foundations.

If the house is still on a septic tank

If the city sewer already runs in the street along your plot, but the house's septic tank or holding tank does not meet environmental requirements, Cabinet Regulation No. 384 obliges the owner to connect the house to the city network. In Šampēteris, where the networks were extended back in phase 3, this can apply to houses still using a septic tank or holding tank.

A connection needs technical conditions from Rīgas ūdens. They are valid for one year. If a property has several owners, one of them may request the conditions, but the design must be agreed with all the co-owners.

This is especially important in a terraced house: if several units share an outlet or inlet, the work affects all the neighbours and is planned together.

Sources: Cabinet Regulation No. 384 in the Rīgas ūdens explanation of co-financing; validity of the conditions and agreement of co-owners: Rīgas ūdens FAQ. Checked 05.11.2026.

1990s terraced houses: plumbing around 30 years old

Šampēteris has 1990s terraced houses. Each unit may have its own inlet and outlet, or several units may share them, and it is worth finding out before you change anything. The plumbing in these houses is now around 30 years old: the pipes, mixer taps and water heaters (boilers) of that time have reached the age when they start leaking more often.

  • If the original water heater leaks or no longer heats, replacement is often better value than repair; water heater installation starts from €45.
  • Mixer taps with a worn cartridge drip; mixer tap repair from €25.
  • If units share a sewer outlet, a blockage in one unit affects the neighbours too.

Gardens around detached houses and standard blocks at the junction

Mixed-use areas take up 58.7% of the district, residential areas 18%, and the detached houses have gardens. A typical plumbing job in a garden is the outdoor tap and watering supply, which must be drained before the frost so that the pipe does not split inside the wall.

In the multi-storey blocks at the junction of Irlavas, Stendes and Zalves streets and in the 1980s blocks of apartments, the jobs are different: the apartments' supply pipes up to the riser, toilet and bath replacement, leaks and blockages. Riser work there is agreed with the house manager.

Shallow groundwater by the Jūrmala railway

In most of Šampēteris the groundwater lies 1.5–3 m deep, but in the central and eastern parts near the Jūrmala railway it is shallower than 1.5 m. In this strip basements and plant rooms are damper, and when digging to repair a supply pipe or outlet, water quickly collects in the pit.

If there is a pump in the basement, check it before autumn: the float must move freely and the outlet pipe must be clear. Sump pump repair starts from €75.

Low pressure in a detached house: where to look

If the pressure in a detached house has dropped over the years, the cause is often in the house itself: a clogged strainer at the inlet, a corroded or not fully open valve, deposits in old pipes. If only one tap runs weakly, the fault is in it or its supply pipe; if it is the whole house, we check the inlet and the first valve after it.

Low water pressure diagnostics start from €25. If the pressure is low along the whole street, it is not a matter for the house's plumbing, and it should be reported to Rīgas ūdens.
