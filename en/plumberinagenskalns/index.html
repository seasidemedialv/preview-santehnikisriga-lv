Plumber in Āgenskalns, Riga

A plumber in Āgenskalns meets three very different types of building: protected 19th-century wooden housing, perimeter masonry blocks along Nometņu iela and Slokas iela, and Riga's first microdistrict, “Āgenskalna priedes”. The ground differs too, from dry dunes to silt near Victory Park. Below is how these differences change plumbing work.

Wooden housing between Nometņu iela and E. Smiļģa iela

The area between Nometņu iela and E. Smiļģa iela is an urban planning monument of national importance with a heritage of wooden architecture. Most of the buildings date from the first half of the 19th century, because the earlier ones were burnt down in 1812. Low-rise apartment houses with gardens dominate the small streets.

In a house like this, water supply and sewerage were put in long after it was built, often one apartment at a time. That is why you find shared inlets for several apartments, pipes under board floors and sewer pipes running through the basement or a crawl space under the house.

In a house where the wooden structure itself is valuable, we plan the pipe route so that load-bearing beams do not have to be drilled and historic finishes are not damaged. We leave joints where they can be reached without lifting the floor.

A house with a garden: what belongs to the house and what to Rīgas ūdens

A small apartment house with a garden often has no house manager on hand every day, but the duties do not disappear. Part two of Section 6 of the Residential Property Management Law (Dzīvojamo māju pārvaldīšanas likums) lists water supply and sewerage among the mandatory management tasks, together with inspection, maintenance and routine repair of the utility lines.

Rīgas ūdens is responsible up to the ownership boundary. Usually this is the outer edge of the wall of the metering chamber, or, if there is no chamber, the red line of the street. The inlet through the garden to the house and the outlet to the street network are the owners' responsibility, and leaks in them must be fixed at their own cost.

If several apartments share one inlet, we agree the time of the work with all of them, because the water has to be shut off for the whole house during the repair.

Sources: Residential Property Management Law, Section 6, part two (likumi.lv); ownership boundary: the Rīgas ūdens page on service responsibility boundaries. Checked 05.11.2026.

“Āgenskalna priedes”: a microdistrict from the 1950s and 1960s on a dune

In the 1950s and 1960s Riga's first microdistrict, “Āgenskalna priedes”, was built on a dune. These houses are older than the later mass-produced series, so in many apartments the branch pipes have already been replaced, but the risers not always.

A typical job here is connecting a new apartment branch pipe to an old riser. The transition between a new plastic pipe and an old metal riser is exactly where leaks most often start, so we choose the type of joint according to the material and condition of the old pipe. If the building is preparing to replace its risers, it is worth timing the apartment work to match.

Māra's Pond, Victory Park and the dry dunes

Towards Victory Park (Uzvaras parks) and Klīversala, building conditions are poor: sand alternates with layers of silt 3–10 m thick, and groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m. It is just as shallow around Māra's Pond (Māras dīķis). On the Āgenskalns dunes in the centre conditions are good, and north of Kalnciema iela groundwater is deeper than 3 m.

  • Near Māra's Pond and Victory Park, groundwater can push into the basement through the seams around the inlet or outlet; first we establish whether the water comes from the ground or from a pipe.
  • On silt, buildings and pipes settle unevenly, so an old sewer pipe in the basement can lose its fall or separate at a joint.
  • On the dry dunes such problems are rarer, and the ground usually does not get in the way of repairing the outside inlet.

If the basement has a pump that pumps out water, sump pump repair costs from €75.

Perimeter masonry buildings along Nometņu iela and Slokas iela

Along Nometņu iela and Slokas iela the buildings form perimeter blocks, as in the centre: multi-storey masonry houses around courtyards. Their plumbing issues are similar to those in other pre-war buildings: bathrooms rebuilt in different years, risers built into walls and sewerage from the whole house collected into one outlet on the courtyard side.

If water rises in a ground-floor sink or toilet when the neighbours upstairs use water, the shared outlet is blocked, not your trap. Drain cleaning starts from €40; cleaning of the shared outlet is agreed with the house manager.

Frequently asked questions

The section from the ownership boundary to the house is the joint responsibility of the owners, and its upkeep is organised by the house manager or a person the owners choose. For a repair the water has to be shut off for the whole house, so agree the time with your neighbours.
Around Māra's Pond groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m, so the damp often comes from the ground. If the damp is the same all year round or the water is warm, we check the inlet and the sewer outlet.
Most often it is the transition between the new pipe and the old riser that leaks. We redo it with a joint suited to the riser material; if the riser itself is damaged, the work is agreed with the house manager.
If a pipe runs through an unheated basement or crawl space by an outside wall, it can freeze. Insulation or moving the pipe to a warmer place helps; if it has already frozen, thawing costs from €55.
If it happens when water is used upstairs, the shared outlet that belongs to the whole house is blocked. Report it to the house manager; we can do the cleaning once it is agreed with them.
