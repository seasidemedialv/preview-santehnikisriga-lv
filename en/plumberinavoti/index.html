Plumber in Avoti, Riga

A plumber in Avoti mostly works in pre-war buildings: five- and six-storey masonry tenement houses and old wooden houses where the pipes have been relaid piece by piece over a hundred years. Avoti is also one of the places where two water sources meet in the city network. Here we explain what this means for your taps, pipes and renovation plan.

Late Art Nouveau tenement houses: pipes replaced piece by piece

Avoti is characterised by late Art Nouveau masonry tenement houses of five or six storeys. In a building this old the plumbing has almost never been replaced in one go: on one floor the riser is already plastic, on another it is still steel or cast iron, and bathrooms in the apartments were put in at different times and in different places.

In practice this means many transitions between different materials and pipes that take unexpected routes, for example through a former maid's room or a corner of the kitchen. Before we quote a price, we find out where the pipe comes from, what it is made of and whether the joint can be opened without damaging the old section.

In thick masonry walls and floor slabs, pipes are often built in. If there is a leak inside a wall, we first establish which water is leaking (cold, hot or sewage) and where the centre of the damp is, and only then open the wall over the smallest possible area.

Wooden houses around Avotu iela

Around Avotu iela, low wooden buildings dominated in the second half of the 19th century, and in many places they have survived, mostly in poor condition. In these houses water supply and sewerage were installed much later than the building itself, and the pipes often run along the floor or an outside wall.

  • A pipe along a cold outside wall or in an unheated corridor can freeze in a frost; thawing frozen pipes costs from €55.
  • A sewer pipe under a wooden floor loses its fall over time as the floor settles, and the water starts to drain slowly or stand.
  • If the timber structure is already damp, the leak must be fixed immediately: wood soaks up water quickly but dries slowly.

In a wooden house it is worth planning the pipe route so that the joints stay accessible rather than hidden under the floorboards.

Where the apartment's pipes end in an old tenement house

Section 3 of the Apartment Property Law (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums) states that an apartment includes the utility lines up to the risers that are common property, while Section 4 counts the systems serving the whole building as common property. In a pre-war building this boundary is not always visible, because the riser may be hidden in a wall or run through a neighbour's apartment.

In everyday terms: the branch from the riser to your mixer tap, toilet or bath is the apartment owner's concern, and we can replace it. Replacing a section of the riser is agreed with the building or its house manager. If the building is preparing to replace all the risers, it is better value to replace the apartment branches at the same time; how such costs are shared is explained in the article who pays for riser replacement.

Source: Apartment Property Law, Section 3, part two, and Section 4, part one (likumi.lv). Checked 05.11.2026.

Water from Baltezers and the Daugava meets in Avoti

About half of Riga's water comes from the Baltezers groundwater reservoir, and the other half is treated from the Daugava. RTU measurements from 2021 show that the two waters meet in the pipes around Avotu iela, and in such places the risk of the water turning cloudy is higher.

From 29.09.2026 to 02.10.2026, Rīgas ūdens flushed iron and mineral deposits out of an 8.5 km trunk main between Cēsu iela and the Dreiliņi roundabout, and Avoti was among the districts warned that the water might turn cloudy.

In an old building cloudy water also carries rust from the building's own pipes. It settles in the screens of mixer taps, in thermostatic valves and in the water heater (boiler). If a tap runs weakly after such days, start by cleaning the aerator; if that does not help, the fault may be in the cartridge, and replacing it is part of mixer tap repair, which starts from €25.

Water sources: LSM (2018) on the Baltezers and Daugava shares, LV portāls on the RTU measurements from 2021 and on the trunk main flushing in autumn 2026. Checked 05.11.2026.

A bathroom in a pre-war building: what to find out before renovation

In Avoti buildings the bathroom is often set up in a former pantry or part of the kitchen, far from the riser. A long sewer branch with little fall is the main reason water drains slowly in such rooms.

  • The position and fall of the outlet determine where the toilet, shower or bath can go.
  • On a wooden floor, a shower floor drain and waterproofing need a different solution than on concrete.
  • If the riser is still cast iron, the transition to plastic pipe is chosen specifically for that material.

Bathroom plumbing costs from €200; the price depends on the length of the pipes, removal of the old fittings and whether the outlet has to be moved.
