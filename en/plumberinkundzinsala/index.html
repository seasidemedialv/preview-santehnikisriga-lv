Plumber in Kundziņsala, Riga

Our plumber in Kundziņsala works in an unusual place: a small residential area sits in the south-east of the island, surrounded on all sides by the Freeport of Riga. Few people live here, and part of the plumbing is in buildings within the port area. The groundwater is shallow across the whole island, and that shapes much of the work.

A residential area in the middle of the port

The Kundziņsala residential area covers 34.7 ha in the south-east of the island and is zoned for mixed development. The district is an enclave within the Freeport of Riga: the port complex has operated here since 1981, and until 1959 the island could only be reached by boat or over the ice. In 2025 the island had 331 residents.

For plumbing this means two quite different kinds of customer: home owners in the residential area, and port buildings with their own washrooms. In both cases the work is household-level, but access and approvals differ.

The houses in the residential area are of different ages, and so is their plumbing. Before a repair we find out where the main valve of the house is, what the pipes are made of and where the outlet runs, because that decides how much work even a small leak will take.

Shallow groundwater and flood risk across the island

Across the whole of Kundziņsala the groundwater is shallower than one and a half metres. In the northern part and along the shore the flood risk is at least 10%, and in the residential area it is above 1%.

  • Basements and low plant rooms need a sump pump with a correctly set float; sump pump repair from €75.
  • When an inlet or sewer outlet is repaired, the trench quickly fills with groundwater, so it is pumped out.
  • If the drains in the house only run slowly during heavy rain or high water, we check whether water is getting into the outlet from outside.

Water heaters (boilers), pumps and other electrical appliances in the lowest rooms are safer when raised above the floor.

Utilities: what is not publicly documented

We found no public sources saying whether the houses in the Kundziņsala residential area are connected to the city water supply and sewer. So we make no claim about any particular house.

If a house is connected, Rīgas ūdens is responsible up to the ownership boundary: usually the wall of the meter chamber, or the street's red line if there is no chamber. Everything beyond it is maintained by the owner. If the house uses its own well or a local sewage system, the whole route of water and waste water is the owner's own responsibility.

Ownership boundary: the Rīgas ūdens page on service responsibility boundaries. Checked 05.11.2026.

Buildings in the port area

In port buildings our work is the same household plumbing as in homes: toilet and cistern repair, sinks, water heaters and leaks. Toilet repair starts from €30, water heater repair from €45.

If the building is managed by a company, the request can come from the property manager; more on the plumber for property managers page. In the request, say how to reach the site within the port area.

In buildings with many users the cistern mechanism, traps and mixer taps wear out faster, and it pays to replace them on a schedule rather than after a breakdown.
