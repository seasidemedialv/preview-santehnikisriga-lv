Plumber in Mežciems, Riga

Our plumber in Mežciems works mainly in panel blocks built in 1975–1986: the five-storey 466 series, the nine- and sixteen-storey 602 series and the 104 series buildings. What these blocks have in common is that their original pipes have served longer than they were designed to. Here is what that means for risers, leaks and bathroom renovation.

Three series in one estate

The Mežciems high-rise estate was built in one go. The large-panel buildings of the 5-storey 466 series, the 9- and 16-storey 602 series and the 104 series stand side by side, and each has its own bathroom layout.

For a plumber this means that the position of the risers, the size of the shaft and the access differ even between neighbouring blocks. In one block the riser is behind a removable panel, in another it is tiled over, and that decides how much work even a small repair will take. In a sixteen-storey building, a leak at the top can also reach many floors below. So when a shaft or box is opened, it is worth checking the nearby riser joints straight away, not just the one damaged spot.

The building lasts 70 years, the pipes much less

In the LV portāls summary of Annex 2 to Cabinet Regulation No. 906, the 104 and 602 series are in group 5 with a service life of 70 years; the 466 series is not named there. The norm for pipes is much shorter: 15–30 years for cold water and sewer pipes, depending on the pipe type, and 60 years only for lines with cast-iron fittings.

The Mežciems blocks are now between 40 and 51 years old. If the pipes have not been replaced since construction, the water pipes have exceeded their standard life, and the cast-iron drains are getting close to it. That is why leaks most often start at joints, at transitions to plastic and where a pipe passes through a floor slab.

Source: LV portāls explanation of Cabinet Regulation No. 906 (service lives of buildings and building services). Checked 05.11.2026.

Risers in Mežciems: who decides and who pays

A riser does not belong to any one apartment. It is part of the building's common property, and the decision on replacing it and paying for it is taken by the owners' meeting. A single apartment owner can only replace the section within their own floor, and even that is agreed with the house manager, because the riser has to be shut off and drained for the duration of the work.

If the owners' meeting in a 602 series block has decided to replace the risers, it pays for the apartment owner to replace their own supply pipes from the riser to the taps at the same time. Riser replacement starts from €100 per riser within one floor; the price depends on whether cast iron has to be cut out, whether the riser is in a tiled box and how many risers are replaced at once. A leak in a nine- or sixteen-storey block

In a tall building one damaged joint can flood several apartments below you. The first step is to close the apartment's inlet valve; if the leak is before it or in the riser itself, tell the house manager at once; they will shut off the riser.

  • The washing machine or dishwasher supply hose: a common cause after a kitchen or bathroom renovation.
  • A dripping joint under the bath or behind the toilet that nobody has seen for years.
  • A crack in the old cast-iron sewer tee by the riser.

Flooded apartment: emergency help from €45. If the damage has to be recorded for the insurer, flood documentation costs from €70. We contact you within 5 minutes of your request.

The Šmerļupīte, the former Hausmanis bog and the detached-house fringes

Along the banks of the Šmerļupīte the groundwater is shallower than one and a half metres, and the south-west of the district is the site of the former Hausmanis bog. When the estate was built, the Dreiliņupīte stream was linked to the Šmerļupīte by a pipeline under S. Eizenšteina Street. In the centre, north-east and east, conditions are favourable.

For blocks of apartments in the shallow groundwater strip this means damper basements, where the mains and outlets run. Detached houses occupy 11.8 ha on the western edge and in the north-east by Malienas Street; there the work is as in any private house, and in a basement with water a sump pump helps (repair from €75).
