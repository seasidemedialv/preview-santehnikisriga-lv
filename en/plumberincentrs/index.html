These buildings are older than their water supply, so the plumbing in them has been fitted around the walls and courtyards, not the other way round. Masonry tenement houses in the boulevard ring and beyond

When the city fortifications were demolished in the 19th century, solid masonry buildings began to go up in the boulevard ring, and similar buildings continue further into the centre's blocks. The historic centre with its Art Nouveau and wooden architecture was added to the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1998.

The water supply and sewerage in these buildings have been rebuilt many times. In one apartment you may find a cast iron sewer section, steel water pipes and plastic pipes from a recent renovation. Leaks most often happen exactly where different materials are joined with adaptors, so before the work we find out what is inside the wall and under the floor.

Often the bathroom has been fitted in a room not originally meant for it, well away from the riser. A long horizontal sewer run needs enough fall, otherwise sediment builds up in it and the water drains more and more slowly.

In a historic building: courtyards, stairs and common property

In an old building it is better to plan plumbing work inside the apartment. If the work affects the facade, the stairwell, the basement or another part of the common property, it is first agreed with the house manager.

If the floor between storeys is timber, a leak is particularly unpleasant: the water soaks into the beams and shows on the neighbour's ceiling with a delay. So even a small dripping joint under the bath must be fixed straight away, not left until the next renovation.

In courtyard wings and on upper floors, access tends to be narrow. Before buying a new bath or a large shower cabin, measure the doors, the turns of the stairs and the bathroom entrance, so that the unit can actually reach its place.

Shallow groundwater by the station and Valdemāra iela

Around the boulevard ring, the Central Station and Valdemāra iela building conditions are difficult, and in part of the area groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m. In the central part it is 1.5–3 m deep. In the valley of the City Canal the water level rises and falls with storm surges, but elsewhere there is no real flood risk.

For a building this usually means a damp basement where the shared sewer and the water inlet run. A shop, storeroom or laundry room set up in the basement may need a sump pit. If the pump switches on too late or hums without pumping the water out, sump pump repair costs from €75.

In a damp basement cast iron pipes and steel brackets rust faster. If the shared sewer in the basement is leaking, it is a matter for the building, and the first step is to tell the house manager.

Cloudy water around Tērbatas iela

In autumn 2026 (29.09–02.10), Rīgas ūdens flushed the trunk water main on the section between Cēsu iela and the Dreiliņi roundabout, 8.5 km in total. The announcement about possible cloudiness also mentioned the centre, around Tērbatas iela. Flushing carries iron and mineral deposits out of the pipes, and some of them reach the building network through the inlet.

  • One tap runs more weakly afterwards: unscrew and rinse the aerator.
  • The aerator is clean, but the pressure is still low: the filter at the apartment inlet or the cartridge may be clogged; mixer tap repair from €25.
  • The water heater (boiler) starts hissing or heats more slowly: sediment collects at the bottom of the tank, and water heater cleaning costs from €35.

If all the water throughout the building is cloudy, the apartment plumbing will not solve it. Let the cold water run and, if nothing changes, tell the house manager.

Inlet valve, riser and the house manager's duties

In an old building it is often unclear where the apartment's plumbing ends. In its judgment of 21.09.2023 in case SKC-535/2023, the Senate ruled that the water inlet valve fitted at the apartment inlet is inseparably linked to the common property utility lines and is part of the building's shared water supply system.

Section 6 of the Residential Property Management Law (Dzīvojamo māju pārvaldīšanas likums) counts water supply and sewerage among the mandatory management tasks, together with inspection, maintenance and routine repair of the utility lines. If the valve, the riser or the basement sewer is leaking, write to the house manager. The pipes, taps, toilet and drains beyond the valve are your responsibility, and we can repair or replace them.

The inlet valve as part of the building's shared system: Senate judgment of 21.09.2023 in case SKC-535/2023. House manager's duties: Residential Property Management Law, Section 6, part 2 (likumi.lv). Checked 05.11.2026.
