Plumber in Vecāķi, Riga

A plumber in Vecāķi works in detached houses among the dunes: some are old wooden summer houses, others are newer homes. The groundwater in the dunes is deep, but wooden houses built for summer living need different plumbing in winter. On this page we explain what matters here.

Summer houses since 1898 and modern detached homes

From 1898 plots of land for summer houses were granted in Vecāķi, and by 1914 there were about 100 summer houses here, wooden buildings typical of Jūrmala. Today detached housing is the dominant land use, covering 39.7% or 91.4 ha.

If an old wooden house is now lived in during the winter as well, its plumbing has to be reviewed from scratch. In a summer house the pipes may run through an unheated crawl space, along an outer wall or under the veranda, and there they freeze in frost.

  • Pipes in an unheated crawl space or under the floor are moved into the warm zone or insulated.
  • For the inlet we check whether it is laid deep enough and whether the point where it enters the building is insulated.
  • The water heater (boiler) and the bathroom plumbing are placed in a room where the temperature does not drop below zero.

Networks in Vecāķi and the ownership boundary

Vecāķi is named in the Rīgas ūdens project "Water Management Development in Riga, Phases 1–2" (1996–2006), which renewed and extended the water supply and sewer networks. In the Mangaļsala project the new water main is planned to be connected to the Vecāķi trunk networks.

Rīgas ūdens must provide the service up to the ownership boundary: usually this is the outer edge of the wall of the metering chamber, or, if there is no chamber, the street's red line. Beyond it the owner maintains the inlet and the sewer connection and repairs leaks at their own expense.

In a summer house that was used only in summer for years, this very section tends to be the oldest and the least documented. Before a conversion it is worth finding out where it runs and what material it is made of.

Sandy dunes and low ground near Vecdaugava

The relief of Vecāķi is broken up by dunes, the highest being the Vaļņa dune (18.9 m). In the south-eastern and eastern parts the groundwater lies at least 1.5 m deep, and in the centre deeper than 3 m. Historically, shifting dunes caused damage here, and in the southern and western parts near Vecdaugava the ground is only 2–4 m above sea level.

In the dunes a trench usually stays dry, but the sand is loose, and a pipe needs a compacted bed so that it does not settle. In the low part near Vecdaugava a basement or plant room can be damp, and a sump pump helps there (repair from €75).

Water hardness and the water heater

Rīgas ūdens states that water hardness is up to 2 mmol/l; this is one figure for the whole city, not by district. Over time limescale builds up on the water heater's element and in the tank, and the water heats up more slowly.

In a house used on and off, the water heater has to be prepared for winter: if the house stands unheated for a long time, drain it, otherwise the tank can freeze and burst. Water heater repair costs from €45, cleaning from €35.

Sewerage in an old wooden house

In a wooden summer house the sewer pipes often run under the floor in the crawl space, where it is cold in winter. If a pipe sags and water stands in it, it freezes in frost and the pipe can burst. So when an old house is converted, we check not only the material but also whether the pipes have an even fall along their whole length.

In a house that has stood empty, the water in the traps dries out and a sewer smell comes into the room through them. Usually it is enough to pour water into all the drains. If the smell does not go away, the cause may be a damaged seal or vent pipe, and smell diagnostics cost from €35.
