Pārdaugava's wooden housing and plumbing added later

By 1819 around 1,000 low-rise wooden houses had been built on the left bank (Pārdaugava), and Torņakalns is part of this historic housing zone. Mixed-use areas take up 43.3% of the district, or 139.1 ha, so one block can contain apartments, an office and a workshop.

In buildings put up before the age of running water, the toilet and bathroom are often fitted into a former pantry or by the stairs, and the drains run along a long horizontal section to the outlet. A long section needs an even fall. If it has sagged somewhere, water stands there and deposits build up until drainage becomes slow throughout the house.

In wooden structures a leak is not just a damp mark. If water drips under the floor for a long time, the joists soak up the moisture, and the repair afterwards costs far more than the leak itself. So here it is worth reacting to the first smell or dark patch: pipe repair starts from €35.

A house or an apartment in an old building: where your responsibility ends

If you own the whole house, Rīgas ūdens is responsible only up to the ownership boundary. Most often this is the outer edge of the wall of the metering chamber; if there is no chamber, the street's red line. The inlet across the yard to the house and everything in the house are yours.

If the old building is divided into apartments, the Apartment Property Law (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums) applies: the apartment owns the utility networks up to the common risers (Section 3(2)), while the systems that serve the whole building are common property (Section 4(1)). In a wooden house the riser may be just one pipe running through the kitchens of two apartments, but it is still common property.

In a mixed-use block a single building often has both apartments and premises let to an office or workshop. If a tenant wants a new sink or kitchenette, the connection point is chosen so that the new pipe does not worsen the fall in the shared outlet, which is already long.

Ownership boundary: Rīgas ūdens, responsibility boundaries for water services. Boundary between apartment and common property: Sections 3 and 4 of the Apartment Property Law (likumi.lv). Checked 05.11.2026.

By Māra's Pond and on the Torņakalns dunes

In the western part of the district around Māra's Pond building conditions are difficult, and the groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m. In the south-west on the Torņakalns dunes the groundwater is 1.5–3 m deep and deeper in places. There is a flood risk only by the Kīleveins ditch, up to 2%.

Smells and slow drainage in an old building

In old buildings a sewer smell is often caused not by a blockage but by a dried-out trap in a rarely used sink, a leaky joint between cast iron and plastic, or a riser without venting. Smell diagnostics cost from €35, and during them we find out where the smell is coming from.

If drainage is slow in only one place, cleaning helps; if it is the whole house, the fault is usually in the long horizontal section or the outlet. Drain cleaning starts from €40.
