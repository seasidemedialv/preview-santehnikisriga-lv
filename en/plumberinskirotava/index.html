Plumber in Šķirotava, Riga

A plumber in Šķirotava is most often called to a business building, warehouse or workshop, less often to a house in the south of the district. The work is similar to that in a residential building, but the questions differ: who owns the building, who is responsible for the pipe and how to bring water to a building that has never had it.

The railway marshalling yard and mixed development

In Šķirotava, technical development areas, mainly the railway marshalling tracks, take up 14.3%, and mixed-use areas 24.8%. In the southern part of the district the city plan also allows detached houses.

In a warehouse or industrial building the plumbing is often scattered: a toilet block in the office, a sink in the workshop, an outdoor tap by the gate. Long pipes in unheated rooms are the main risk in winter, while blockages usually happen where things go into the drains that do not belong there: sand, oil or building debris.

If part of the building is not heated in winter, it is worth separating the water supply there with its own stop valve and drain cock. Then in the cold season that section can be drained while the rest of the building keeps its water. That is cheaper than repairing a burst pipe after the first frost.

A burst pipe in a business building: who is responsible

Rīgas ūdens explains that if a pipe bursts or the sewer blocks inside a building, the repair is the building owner's responsibility. Rīgas ūdens is responsible only up to the building's metering chamber or the building's outer wall.

If you rent the premises, first notify the building owner or property manager, and check the lease for who pays for plumbing emergencies. In the emergency itself, the most important thing is knowing where the building's main stop valve is. If nobody knows, it is worth finding out now, not when water is already running.

We also work evenings, nights and weekends when a plumber is free. An emergency plumber call-out costs from €35, burst pipe repair from €55.

Responsibility of the building owner and Rīgas ūdens: Rīgas ūdens, frequently asked questions. Checked 05.11.2026.

A new water or sewer connection for a building

For a building that has never had water or a sewer, the first document is the Rīgas ūdens technical conditions under paragraph 19 of Cabinet Regulation No. 174. The applicant can be the developer of the construction project or one of the co-owners. The conditions are issued free of charge and take 11 working days to prepare.

Then come the design and the external supply pipe. Inside the building we can install the system up to the fixtures: the toilet block, sinks, water heater (boiler) and drains up to the outlet. On larger projects we also work as a plumbing subcontractor, and we agree the price before the work starts.

Technical conditions for a new connection: Cabinet Regulation No. 174, the Rīgas ūdens page on properties without a connection. Checked 05.11.2026.

Groundwater and the area around Katlakalna Street

In Šķirotava the groundwater is mostly 1.5–3 m deep, in places deeper. Difficult building conditions exist only in the north-west around Katlakalna Street. In most of the district, external sewer and inlet work can therefore be done without special measures against water in the trench.

In the north-western part the situation may be different, and before digging it is worth knowing the ground conditions at the exact spot.

Groundwater depth and difficult conditions: apkaimes.lv, geography of Šķirotava. Checked 05.11.2026.

Detached houses in southern Šķirotava

In a detached house the inlet pipe and the outdoor tap are the first places to freeze in winter, especially if the house has been empty for a while. Drain the outdoor tap before the frosts. If the water has already stopped coming through, thawing frozen pipes costs from €55.
