Plumber in Bišumuiža, Riga

A plumber in Bišumuiža works more often in detached houses and new residential buildings than in large prefab blocks. In part of the district, city water and sewerage with branches up to the plot boundary were built as early as 2011–2015, but not every house uses them. Here we explain how a connection works, what changes by the Bišumuiža ditch and which deadlines have already passed.

Detached houses, new builds and industry by Bauskas iela

Residential and detached-house areas together take up 29% of Bišumuiža. By Bauskas iela and Ceraukstes iela there is mixed development with an industrial character, and since Jāņa Čakstes gatve was built, more new residential houses are going up in the district.

So the work is of three kinds. In older detached houses the inlets and internal sewerage need renewing; in new houses sanitary fittings, water heaters (boilers) and underfloor heating have to be connected; and in industrial and warehouse buildings it is more often toilets, water heaters and leaks that need repair.

Phase 4 networks: the branch up to the boundary is already there

In the Cohesion Fund project “Development of Water Management in Riga, Phase 4” (2011–2015), Rīgas ūdens built new water supply and sewer networks in part of Bišumuiža. In the project documents this area belonged to a district called “Katlakalns”, together with part of Ziepniekkalns. Branches were built up to the boundary of each plot.

A branch at the boundary means the owner only has to build their own section to the house, but putting the inside of the house in order is just as important. Before designing the outside section, technical conditions must be requested from Rīgas ūdens (Cabinet Regulation No. 174, paragraph 19); Rīgas ūdens prepares them within 11 working days free of charge.

If sewerage has been built in the adjacent street but the septic tank or holding tank does not meet environmental requirements, Cabinet Regulation No. 384 obliges the owner to connect to city sewerage.

Networks up to the plot boundary: Rīgas ūdens, EU fund co-financed projects (phase 4). Technical conditions: Cabinet Regulation No. 174, Rīgas ūdens. Obligation to connect: Cabinet Regulation No. 384 (as explained by Rīgas ūdens). Checked 05.11.2026.

Compensation deadlines for phase 4 districts have ended

In the phase 4 districts, Bišumuiža among them, the municipality co-financed the building work for connections: €1,000 for a connection pipe up to 5 m, €1,500 for 5 to 20 m and €2,000 for a longer one. The connection had to be built by 31.12.2025, and the application submitted by 01.04.2026.

Both deadlines have passed. If the house is not yet connected, ask Rīgas ūdens before planning whether any other support is currently available for your property. Without compensation the owner pays the costs, so it matters all the more not to build anything twice: the position and height of the outlet in the house are matched to the design.

Co-financing amounts and deadlines (31.12.2025 for construction, 01.04.2026 for the application): Rīgas ūdens announcement on municipal co-financing of connections. Checked 05.11.2026.

By the Bišumuiža ditch: peat, silt and water in the trench

Around the Bišumuiža ditch building conditions are difficult. Groundwater there is shallower than 1.5 m, and the sand layers contain peat and silt. On the Ziepniekkalns dunes right next door groundwater is deeper than 1.5 m, so two neighbouring plots can be completely different.

  • Water can appear in the trench at a shallow depth, and the excavation has to be pumped out.
  • In peat and silt a pipe without a stable sand bed settles over time and the fall can disappear; then sediment starts to build up in the connection pipe.
  • A sump pump is useful in a low basement or plant room; if it starts switching on wrongly, repair costs from €75.

A new build by Jāņa Čakstes gatve: what to check before the finishing work

In a new house the cheapest moment to fix plumbing mistakes is before the tiles and floor covering. Once the pipes are covered, every repair means opening finishes that are already done.

  • Water pipes are pressure-tested while they are still visible, and the joints are watched during the test.
  • Underfloor heating loops should be of similar length, and the manifold must stay accessible so that the system can be bled and balanced.
  • For sewer pipes under the floor the fall is checked, and cleaning access points are left where the pipe changes direction.
  • Separate shut-off valves are fitted for the water heater and washing machines, so that the whole house does not have to be shut off during a repair.
