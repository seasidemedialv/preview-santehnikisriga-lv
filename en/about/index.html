Where we work

We work only in the city of Riga, in all 58 districts. This site does not accept requests from the municipalities around Riga.

Plumbing work in Riga varies with the type of building. In apartment buildings the question is often the shared risers and where the apartment owner's responsibility ends. In districts of private houses, well pumps, drainage and connections to the city networks come up more often. That is why every district has its own page, for example Purvciems, Mežaparks and Bolderāja.

How the price is made up

We show prices as "from €X", for example mixer tap repair from €25. That is the starting price for a simple job, not a fixed amount for every case.

The final price depends on:

  • the scope of work: one joint or a whole section of pipe;
  • access: a pipe in an open shaft or behind tiles and boxing;
  • materials and parts: these are usually charged separately unless stated otherwise;
  • approval: work on common property, for example risers or radiators, needs the house manager's consent.

We give the exact price once we have assessed the situation and agree it before the work starts. If another fault turns up during the work, we first agree the extra work and its price with you.

How to contact us

Contact is only through the form, which is on every page. We contact you within 5 minutes of your request. We also work evenings, nights and weekends when a plumber is free.

We deliberately have no phone number or e-mail: the form already contains the district, the problem and the urgency, so the conversation is shorter. More on the contact page.

What we do not do

  • Gas work: gas cookers, gas boilers and gas pipes.
  • Water meters: we do not install or replace them.
  • Water filters: we do not offer filter installation as a service.
  • Work outside the city of Riga.

If your request concerns one of these jobs, we will not be able to do it.
