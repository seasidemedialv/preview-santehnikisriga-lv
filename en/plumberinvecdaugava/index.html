Plumber in Vecdaugava, Riga

A plumber in Vecdaugava works on a peninsula where the groundwater is close to the surface, the soils are weak and the flood risk is among the highest in Riga. Most homes are cottages and detached houses from the 1950s and 1960s. Here, what happens to the pipes in the ground and the water in the basement matters more than the taps.

Cottages built in the 1950s and 1960s

Vecdaugava became part of Riga in 1960, and in the 1950s and 1960s it was built up with cottage-type houses. Detached housing areas cover 38.7 ha in total. In many houses the plumbing has been added to several times: a first bathroom, later a second one, a sauna, a water heater (boiler) in the basement.

In such a house, pipes from different years meet in one system. Steel supply pipes clog up from the inside, and the joint to a newer section is where leaks appear most often. If the pressure in the house has dropped gradually, the cause is often part of the old inlet, not a tap.

A peninsula with at least a 10% flood risk

The neighbourhood portal apkaimes.lv gives Vecdaugava one of the worst flooding situations in Riga: across most of the peninsula the risk is at least 10%. Only along Vecāķu prospekts is it 1% in places. The groundwater is mostly shallower than 1.5 m.

  • A basement or pit needs a pump with a float switch and an outlet that takes the water well away from the house. Place the socket for the pump higher than the possible water level.
  • After high water, check whether the pipes in the basement have shifted or settled and whether the bottom of the water heater has been under water.
  • If the basement has flooded and plumbing is damaged, the work can be documented for the insurer: documentation after flooding from €70.

Weak soils: pipes that settle

In most of Vecdaugava the soils are weak. For a sewer pipe this means that without a stable bed it settles over time, and the fall disappears in one spot. Deposits start to build up there, and after a few years the drainage becomes slow, even though the pipe itself is intact.

Cleaning clears such a spot, but the dip remains. If a blockage keeps coming back in the same section, it is time to think about relaying it. Drain cleaning costs from €40, pipe repair from €35; outdoor work in wet ground is best planned for a drier period.

Networks since Phases 1–2 and the duty to connect

Vecdaugava is named in the title of the Rīgas ūdens project "Water Management Development in Riga, Phases 1–2" (1996–2006), during which the water supply and sewer networks were renewed and extended. The project aimed to provide centralised services to at least 95% of Riga residents.

If there is a city sewer in your street but the house still uses a septic tank or holding tank that does not meet environmental requirements, Cabinet Regulation No. 384 requires a connection. In a flood-prone place there is a practical reason as well: a tank that groundwater seeps into fills faster than it is used.

While the local system is in use, Riga Binding Regulations No. 66 require a declaration of the volume of waste water removed to be submitted by 1 May each year. The minimum emptying frequency of a holding tank is set by a formula which, if consumption is not metered, assumes 25 l per declared person per day.

Buying a cottage in Vecdaugava: what to look at

On a flood-prone peninsula, the state of a house's plumbing often tells you how the house has coped with high water. Before buying it is worth looking not only at the taps and the bathroom but also at the basement and what is underground.

  • Whether there is a pump in the basement, whether it switches on by itself, and where the pumped-out water goes.
  • Traces of rust or deposits low down on the pipes and at the bottom of the water heater, which may point to past flooding.
  • Whether the house is connected to the city sewer or uses a tank, and whether there are emptying records.
  • How water flows from all taps at once: weak pressure often points to a clogged old inlet.
