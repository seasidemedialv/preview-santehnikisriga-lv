Water leaking: what to do in the first minutes

While water is leaking, your walls, your floor and the neighbours below all suffer. Stop the water first, then look for a plumber.

  1. 1

    Close the nearest valve

    If the leak is at the toilet, sink or washing machine, turn off the small valve at that appliance.

  2. 2

    Close the inlet valves

    If a pipe is leaking or you cannot tell where the water comes from, turn off the cold and hot water inlet valves. They are usually in the bathroom service area next to the riser.

  3. 3

    Mind the electricity

    If water is reaching the sockets, switch off the power at the panel, provided you can reach it safely over dry floor. Switch off the water heater too.

  4. 4

    Warn the neighbours and fill in the form

    Tell the neighbours below, photograph the damage and briefly describe in the form where it is leaking. We contact you within 5 minutes of your request.

If the valve will not close or the leak is before it, only the house manager or their emergency service can shut off the water: contact them and fill in the form as well. If the water is already on the floor, see the flooded apartment page. We also work evenings, nights and weekends when a plumber is free.

Who is responsible: your pipes, the building's risers or Rīgas ūdens

Who organises and pays for the repair depends on which part is damaged.

What is damagedWho is responsibleWhat to do
Pipes in the apartment after the inlet valve, taps, traps, the toiletThe apartment ownerBook pipe repair or the job you need
Risers, the inlet valve, the building's sewer pipesAll apartment owners together: the owners' meeting decides, the house manager organises the workTell the house manager. On the money: who pays for riser replacement
City networks up to the ownership boundaryRīgas ūdensReport it to the Rīgas ūdens emergency service

The ownership boundary is most often the outer edge of the meter well wall or, where there is no well, the red line of the street. So a burst pipe in an apartment or a blocked sewer in the building is the building owner's responsibility, not that of Rīgas ūdens.

Plumber in Riga: apartments and private houses

A plumber in Riga usually works in one of two settings, and the same job looks different in each.

In an apartment. In series blocks (119, 464, 467, 602 series), common in Purvciems, Imanta, Pļavnieki and Zolitūde, water arrives through shared risers, often behind boxing or tiles. If the job touches a riser, the water is shut off for the whole riser, so the day is agreed with the house manager. The service life of cold water pipes and sewer pipes is 15–30 years depending on type (60 years with cast iron fittings), so before a bathroom renovation it is worth checking the risers too.

In a private house. In the house areas of Mežaparks, Bieriņi, Vecāķi and Teika there is no house manager: beyond the ownership boundary the whole network is the owner's concern. So you more often need well pump repair, a sump pump or plumbing installation for a house. In winter the risk is frost: pipes in an unheated basement or by an outside wall can freeze. You then need thawing, and a seasonal house needs preparing for winter.
