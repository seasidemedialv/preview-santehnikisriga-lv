How to book a job

  1. 1

    Fill in the form

    Enter your name, phone number, district, problem and urgency. It takes about a minute.

  2. 2

    We contact you within 5 minutes

    We contact you within 5 minutes of your request. The first message arrives on WhatsApp to the number you give.

  3. 3

    We agree the time and price

    We clarify the situation, agree a time and give the price. We agree the price before the work starts.

  4. 4

    The work

    The plumber does the work at the agreed time. If another fault turns up during the work, we first agree the extra work and its price with you.

What to enter in the form

  • Name and phone number: required, so that we can contact you.
  • District: choose your district from the list.
  • Address: street, building and apartment. The field is optional, but it helps us plan the work.
  • Problem: choose the closest option, for example leak, blockage, water heater (boiler) or heating. If nothing fits, choose "Other".
  • Urgency: as soon as possible, today, this week or schedule for later.
  • Description: a few words about what is happening, where and since when. The field is optional.

If water is leaking right now, first close the apartment valve and then choose "Emergency" in the form. What to do in an emergency is explained on the emergency plumber page.

Where we work

We work all over Riga, in all 58 districts: from Purvciems and Imanta to Ķengarags, Āgenskalns and the Centre. This site does not accept requests from outside the city of Riga.

You will find all our jobs in the plumbing services section, and starting prices on the plumbing prices page.

Also at night and at weekends

We also work evenings, nights and weekends when a plumber is free. You can send the form at any time, and in the conversation you will find out when the plumber can start the work.
