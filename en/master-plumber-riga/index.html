How to find a reliable plumber

A reliable plumber is not the one who promises to do everything cheaply and at once. It is the one who understands the problem before the work and tells you what the price will depend on. A few signs that help you choose:

  • Names a price or a price range before the work and says what is not included.
  • Asks about the situation: where it leaks, what kind of pipe, whether the water can be shut off, whether there is a photo.
  • Tells you if the job affects the riser or the heating and the house manager needs to know.
  • Does not ask for payment for the whole job in advance, unless expensive parts have to be bought.
  • Gives you an invoice with a description of the work afterwards.

Be wary if you are only given a price after the work, or if it suddenly goes up without explanation. Send a short description through the form, and we contact you within 5 minutes of your request. Our prices are on the page plumbing prices, and we agree the price before the work.

When the law requires a certified specialist

Most everyday plumbing work in an apartment is repair: a mixer tap, a toilet, a trap, a leak at a joint. There are three areas where the regulations require a certified specialist, and they are worth knowing before you book the work.

  • Gas: gas boilers and other gas appliances in an apartment are replaced by a certified specialist, who obtains Gaso's technical conditions before installation. We do not do gas work.
  • Electricity: a building's electrical wiring is installed under the supervision of a certified construction specialist, and inspections are carried out by a certified electrical specialist (building standard LBN 261-15). If a water heater (boiler) or pump needs a new electrical line, an electrician installs it.
  • Construction work: design, construction supervision and expert examination of construction may be carried out by construction specialists with a construction practice certificate (the Construction Law (Būvniecības likums), Section 13). In an apartment building, rebuilding the utility networks may require documents approved by the building authority, for example a certification card.

A construction specialist's certificate can be checked in the register of construction specialists in the Construction Information System. If the job affects the building's risers or heating system, talk to the house manager before the work: who pays for riser replacement, radiator replacement approval.

The Construction Law, Section 13 (construction specialists and the construction practice certificate); LV portāls explanations on installing and inspecting electrical wiring (2025), replacing a gas boiler in an apartment (2021) and documents for rebuilding utility networks (2018). For a specific job, check the requirements with the building authority or Gaso. Checked 05.11.2026.

How to choose a master plumber

Before you book a plumber, a few simple things help you avoid surprises on the invoice.

  • Describe the problem precisely: what is leaking or not working, since when, whether the water is shut off
  • Ask for the price before the work and what it includes: labour, materials, call-out
  • Find out whether the job affects common property (risers, the inlet valve); then the house manager needs to know
  • After a flood, ask for a document describing the work done, for your insurer: flood documentation
  • If gas or electrical work is needed, look for a specialist in that field
