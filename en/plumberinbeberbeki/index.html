Phase 5: the network that has reached Beberbeķi

Beberbeķi is one of three districts where Rīgas ūdens has been building sewerage since 2018 in phase 5 of an EU Cohesion Fund project; the other two are Berģi and Imanta. In total 34.59 km of networks and 18 pumping stations were built, and almost 3,000 residents in north-west Imanta, southern Berģi and Beberbeķi gained the option to connect.

In 2023 Beberbeķi residents showed strong interest in connecting. If the network has been built in your street, you can connect, but the owner has to build the connection pipe from the street to the house and the internal switch-over.

Before connecting, it is worth checking where the existing sewer pipe leaves the house and how deep it is. If the outlet is shallow but the street manhole is far away, the fall may not be enough, and that is solved in the design, not on the building site.

Compensation: building work must be finished by 31.12.2026

In Beberbeķi, as in the other phase 5 and 6 districts, the municipality covers up to €739 for design and €1,800 for building work, and for certain groups of residents the costs are covered in full. There are two deadlines: building work finished no later than 31.12.2026, and the compensation application submitted no later than 01.04.2027.

This page was checked on 05.11.2026, so less than two months remain until the building work deadline. If the design is not ready yet, first find out from Rīgas ūdens what stage your documents are at, and plan the internal work so that it does not hold up the handover of the outside connection pipe.

Sources: Rīgas ūdens pages on EU fund projects and the company's history; compensation: Rīgas ūdens news that owners of residential houses can still apply for co-financing. Checked 05.11.2026. Conditions may change, so check them with Rīgas ūdens before the work.

The old septic tank: until the connection and after

State regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 384) require that where there is city sewerage in the adjacent street, a septic tank that does not meet environmental requirements must be replaced by a connection to the network. In Beberbeķi, where the network exists, an old septic tank or holding tank is therefore no longer a long-term solution.

Until the connection is finished, the tank may only be emptied by a septic hauler listed in the register of the Housing and Environment Department. They issue a supporting document with the address, volume and date, and it is worth keeping.

On the day of the switch-over, the internal sewerage is disconnected from the old tank and connected to the new outlet. Then the old tank is emptied so that no waste water is left in it.

Obligation to connect: Cabinet Regulation No. 384, as explained by Rīgas ūdens. Who may remove waste water and what document you must receive: Riga Binding Regulation No. 66 (likumi.lv). Checked 05.11.2026.

Large two-storey houses next to allotment huts

Housing in Beberbeķi is very mixed: allotments and huts that people also live in during winter stand next to large, mostly two-storey detached houses. Detached-house areas take up 42.6%, or 51.4 ha, in the southern and south-western part of the district.

In a two-storey house with several bathrooms, switching to the city network means collecting all the waste water into one outlet and checking that the upstairs branches are properly vented. In a hut lived in over winter the most common trouble is different: the inlet and pipes are not insulated and freeze in a frost; thawing frozen pipes starts from €55.

Shallow groundwater, but no floods

Building conditions in Beberbeķi are generally good, although groundwater is closer to the surface than 1.5 m. There is practically no flood risk.

For the connection trench this means water collects in the pit even at shallow depth, so the trench may have to be pumped out and the pipe needs a stable bed. If the house has a basement, a sump pump helps keep it dry; sump pump repair starts from €75.
