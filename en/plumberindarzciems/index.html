Plumber in Dārzciems, Riga

A plumber in Dārzciems works in two different settings: 103 series, 119 series and small-family project apartment blocks, and detached-house quarters in the north, east and south-east of the district. In the prefab blocks the main issue is risers and apartment pipes; in detached houses it is yard networks and connecting to city sewerage.

Prefab blocks and detached houses in one district

The apartment blocks of Dārzciems include 103 series, 119 series, small-family project and other designs. Detached houses take up 18%, or 81 ha, in the northern, north-eastern, eastern and south-eastern parts of the district, and all residential development 20.6%.

This decides who pays for what. In an apartment building all the owners maintain the shared networks together; in a detached house everything from the ownership boundary to the tap is one owner's responsibility. So the same symptom, such as slow-draining sewerage, means different work in the two settings.

In a prefab apartment the most common jobs are in the bathroom and toilet. Toilet replacement starts from €60, and the price depends on removing the old toilet, the outlet direction (horizontal or vertical, and whether a flexible connector or adaptor is needed), replacing the cistern supply and an uneven floor. Bath replacement starts from €80.

103 and 119 series: different building life, similar pipe life

In Annex 2 of Cabinet Regulation No. 906 the 119 series is in group 4 with a service life of 60 years, and the 103 series in group 5 with 70 years. For pipes the period is much shorter: 15–30 years for cold water pipes and sewerage depending on the type, and 60 years for sewerage with cast iron fittings.

If the building still has its original pipes, their standard service life has most likely ended regardless of the series. For small-family project buildings this list gives no direct answer, so the documents and actual condition of the specific building have to be checked.

In the apartment we replace the branch pipes up to the riser, but replacing the riser itself is organised by the building. Riser replacement starts from €100 per riser within one floor.

Service life of buildings and pipes: Annex 2 of Cabinet Regulation No. 906, summary by LV portāls. Checked 05.11.2026.

Detached-house networks from phase 3 and the obligation to connect

Dārzciems is named in the title of the third water management development phase itself: in 2006–2009 Rīgas ūdens, with EU fund support, extended the water and sewer networks in Šampēteris and here. So in some detached-house streets the city network may have been in place since then.

If a local system does not meet environmental requirements but city sewerage already exists in the adjacent street, Cabinet Regulation No. 384 obliges the owner to connect the house. If your house still uses a septic tank or holding tank even though there is a pipe in the street, it is worth checking.

Inside the house, a connection needs one sewer outlet with the right fall and the old pipe to the tank disconnected. We build this according to the connection design.

Phase 3 components: Rīgas ūdens, EU fund co-financed projects. Obligation to connect: requirement of Cabinet Regulation No. 384, explained on the Rīgas ūdens website. Checked 05.11.2026.

Made ground under yard pipes

Groundwater in Dārzciems is mostly 1.5–3 m below the surface, but the top layer is often 2–3 m of fill. In the north-eastern part groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m.

In made ground the soil settles unevenly, so a yard sewer pipe can lose its fall over time or a joint can separate. Signs are slow draining from the whole house, a smell in the yard and blockages that keep recurring in one place. Drain cleaning solves it for a while, but if the pipe has sunk, the section has to be relaid.

Houses with a basement in the north-eastern part often benefit from a sump pump: its repair starts from €75.

After the trunk main flushing

From 29.09.2026 to 02.10.2026 Rīgas ūdens flushed the trunk water main from Cēsu iela to the Dreiliņi roundabout, and possible cloudiness was also indicated in Dārzciems. If the taps then run weakly or splutter, clean the aerators first; if the supply is weak throughout the house, low water pressure diagnosis starts from €25.
