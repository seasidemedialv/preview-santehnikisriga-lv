Plumber in Ķīpsala, Riga

A plumber in Ķīpsala works in a small and distinctive district: historic wooden houses on the eastern side of the island and new residential projects on alluvial sand. Few people live here, but the buildings are demanding, and at the northern end of the island the ground is very wet. Here is what this means for plumbing.

Fishermen's houses, wooden tenement houses and the “Ķīpsalas kolekcija”

On Ķīpsala the fishermen's log cottages were in time replaced by two-storey wooden tenement houses and detached houses, and four historic buildings have been restored in the “Ķīpsalas kolekcija” (Ķīpsala Collection). The detached-house areas are in the historic part on the eastern side of the island.

In these houses the plumbing is almost always newer than the building itself, and during restoration it is often installed from scratch. The main task is to run the pipes so that they do not damage the logs and beams and the joints stay within reach.

  • Sewer pipes under a wooden floor are fixed so that they do not lose their fall when the floor moves slightly.
  • The water supply is better routed along internal walls, not along cold outside walls.
  • It is worth giving each wet area its own shut-off valve, so that the water for the whole house does not have to be stopped for a repair.

Alluvial sand and water at the northern end of the island

Ķīpsala consists of an 8–15 m thick layer of alluvial sand with silty interlayers. At the northern end groundwater is very high, closer to the surface than 1.5 m, while elsewhere it is 1.5–3 m. The island has also flooded: in 1786 an ice jam put it under water.

For plumbing this means damp basements and plant rooms in the northern part. If the lowest room has a sewer outlet or water inlet, before a repair we establish whether the damp comes from the ground or from a pipe. Where the basement has a sump pit, the pump must switch on in time; sump pump repair costs from €75.

In silty interlayers pipes and foundations can settle unevenly, so blockages in the outside sewer tend to recur in one and the same place.

A burst pipe or a blocked sewer: which part is yours

Rīgas ūdens explains that repairing a burst pipe in an apartment and clearing a blocked sewer in a building are the building owner's responsibility. The company is only responsible up to the building's metering shaft or the outside wall of the building.

In a detached house or small wooden building this means everything beyond that point is the owner's responsibility: the inlet, pipes, appliances and sewerage up to the outlet. If water is leaking in the basement by the inlet, first close the house's main valve. If the leak is in the street or before the meter, tell Rīgas ūdens.

Few residents, very different properties

In 2025, 752 people lived on Ķīpsala, and the island covers 197.5 ha. The properties here are very different: from restored wooden houses to new apartments, so plumbing work in one street can be completely different from that in the next.

If a property is let, it is useful to check the taps, traps, toilet and branch pipes before the tenant moves in, so that you do not have to find a plumber for every little thing later. Plumber for landlords starts from €30.

New projects on the western side of the island

Residential development is planned on the western side of the island. In a new building, plumbing work differs from the historic part: connecting mixer taps and appliances, wall-hung toilets, showers with a floor drain and water underfloor heating.

In a new home typical problems appear after moving in: an unadjusted cistern valve, a dripping joint behind an appliance or hair collected in the shower drain. Washing machine and dishwasher connection starts from €25, and water underfloor heating installation from €300.
