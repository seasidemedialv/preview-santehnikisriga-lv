Heating system flushing in Riga

Heating system flushing in a private house removes from the radiators and pipes the sludge, rust and deposits that build up in the system water over the years. The sign is radiators that are hot at the top but cold at the bottom, or rooms that warm up unevenly. We give the price after inspection, because it depends on the size of the house, the method and how clogged the system is.

When a heating system needs flushing

  • The bottom of the radiator stays cold although the top is hot: sludge has collected at the bottom.
  • Some radiators warm up well, others hardly at all, and bleeding does not help.
  • When you bleed or drain the water, it is dark or contains rust particles.
  • You can hear gurgling or noise in the system, and it often has to be topped up with water.
  • The system is old, and new radiators or an underfloor heating circuit are being added to it.

Flushing cleans the radiators, pipes and underfloor heating circuits. Boiler servicing is not included in this job.

Hydropneumatic or chemical flushing

Hydropneumatic flushing uses water and pulses of compressed air. These loosen the deposits in the pipes and radiators, and the water flushes them out. The chemical-free method is suitable when the deposits are mainly sludge and loose rust.

In chemical flushing, a special agent circulates in the system for a while to break down the deposits, then it is neutralised and the system is flushed out with clean water. It is used when the deposits are hard or the system is heavily clogged.

If only one or two radiators are clogged, they can be removed and flushed separately. That is a smaller job than flushing the whole system.

How heating system flushing works

  1. 1

    Inspection

    We find out the floor area of the house, the number of radiators and underfloor heating circuits, the pipe material and the symptoms. The method is chosen based on this.

  2. 2

    Preparing the system

    The heating is switched off, the system is cooled down and the flushing unit is connected. If there is antifreeze in the system, it is collected.

  3. 3

    Flushing

    The system is flushed section by section or circuit by circuit until the water coming out runs clean.

  4. 4

    Refilling

    The system is refilled with water or antifreeze and, if agreed, a corrosion inhibitor is added.

  5. 5

    Bleeding and testing

    The radiators are bled, the pressure and joints are checked, and after start-up we make sure the radiators warm up evenly.

An apartment with central heating

In an apartment building with central heating, the radiators and risers are part of the whole building's heating system. The Apartment Property Law (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums) also counts heating elements whose operation depends on the shared utilities as common property. That is why flushing the building's system is organised by the house manager, not by an individual apartment owner.

If one radiator in your apartment heats poorly and you are going to replace it, see radiator replacement, which describes getting approval from the house manager.

Source: Section 4, Paragraph 1, Clause 1 of the Apartment Property Law (likumi.lv). Checked on 05.11.2026.

How much does heating system flushing cost?

There is no single starting price for heating system flushing, because the amount of work varies several times over from one house to another. We give the price after inspection and agree it before the work starts. It depends on:

  • the floor area of the house and the number of radiators;
  • the method: hydropneumatic flushing is usually cheaper than chemical;
  • how clogged the system is and the condition of the pipes;
  • the number of underfloor heating circuits;
  • whether the system then has to be filled with antifreeze or an inhibitor added.

The price does not include chemical flushing agents and inhibitor, unless agreed, or boiler servicing. If a radiator or pipe has to be replaced after flushing, we agree it separately: pipe repair from €35, radiator replacement from €70. Heating system refilling and pressure

A closed heating system in a private house runs at a set pressure, shown on the gauge by the boiler or the manifold. If the pressure keeps dropping and you have to top up the system with water, there is a leak somewhere or the expansion vessel is faulty. If the pressure rises sharply as the system heats up, the expansion vessel is short of air or too small.

After flushing or a repair, the system is refilled slowly so the air has time to escape, and then every radiator is bled, starting from the lowest floor. After the first heat-up the pressure is checked again, because more air comes out. The right pressure is set by the boiler and system manufacturer, not by one figure for every house.

We do not adjust or service the boiler itself as part of this work. If a radiator stays cold after refilling, see radiator repair. We agree the price before the work.

Balancing the heating system

Balancing means adjusting how much hot water goes to each radiator or underfloor loop. In an unbalanced system the water takes the easiest route: radiators close to the boiler are hot, while the far ones and those upstairs warm up poorly, even though the system is clean and bled.

  • On radiators, the return valves or the thermostatic valve settings are adjusted.
  • On underfloor heating loops, the flow is adjusted at the flow meters on the manifold.
  • Balancing is worth doing after flushing, after adding new radiators or after installing underfloor heating.

If a loop does not heat at all, balancing will not help and the cause lies elsewhere: underfloor heating repair. We give the price after an inspection, because it depends on the number of radiators and loops.
