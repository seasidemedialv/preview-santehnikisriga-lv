Bidet installation in Riga

Bidet installation is a two-part job: the bidet has to be fixed to the floor or a frame, and it needs a cold and hot water supply and a waste connection. We install floor-standing bidets and wall-hung bidets on a concealed frame, together with the mixer tap and trap. Bidet installation costs from €60, and we agree the price before the work starts.

How a bidet differs from a toilet

A toilet needs a cold water supply and a 110 mm waste opening. In terms of connections a bidet is closer to a sink: it needs cold and hot water points with valves, a mixer tap on the rim of the bidet or on the wall, and a trap that connects to a smaller diameter waste pipe.

That is why in an old bathroom, where there is only a cold water supply next to the toilet, new points have to be installed before the bidet goes in. This is the biggest factor in the price, and it is usually done together with a renovation: plumbing rough-in.

Floor-standing or wall-hung bidet

  • Floor-standing bidet: fixed to the floor with screws, and the points can be in the wall above the floor. It can also be installed after the renovation, when the tiles are already on.
  • Wall-hung bidet: hangs on a concealed frame built into the wall behind plasterboard or in masonry. The frame already has the mounting studs and space for the points, but it must be installed before the wall is closed up and tiled.
  • A bidet frame is not a toilet frame: it has a different waste outlet and space for two water supplies, so a bidet cannot be hung on a toilet frame.
  • If there is a wall-hung toilet next to it, both frames are set at the same height and in line, and this is measured before the wall is closed up.

If the frame is already built in and the wall is tiled, installation is quick: the bidet is hung, and the mixer tap and trap are connected. If the frame still has to be installed, the work is planned together with the tiler, because the position of the frame determines the tile cuts.

A bidet shower if there is no room for a bidet

In a small toilet room a bidet often does not fit. Then a bidet shower is installed next to the toilet: a mixer tap with a hand shower on the wall or a concealed set. It also needs a cold and hot water supply, but no separate waste connection.

For a bidet shower mixer it is worth choosing a model with a shut-off valve or a thermostat, because the hose stays under pressure between uses. It is convenient to connect it together with a new toilet, see toilet installation.

How bidet installation works

  1. 1

    Checking the points

    We check that the cold and hot water points with valves and the waste opening are in the right place and at the right height for the bidet model.

  2. 2

    Mixer tap and trap on the bidet

    Before the bidet is put in place, the mixer tap with flexible supply hoses, the waste valve and the trap are fitted to it, because they are hard to reach later.

  3. 3

    Positioning

    A floor-standing bidet is fixed to the floor, a wall-hung one is hung on the frame studs, and it is levelled with a spirit level.

  4. 4

    Connection

    The supply hoses are connected to the valves and the trap to the waste pipe, and the joint with the floor or wall is sealed.

  5. 5

    Testing

    The water is turned on, and the hot and cold water, the waste valve and all joints are checked for leaks.
