Repairs, alterations and seasonal work

Some jobs are neither an emergency nor a simple installation. They are repairs where the cause has to be found, and alterations where the pipes have to be planned before the finishing work. Each has its own page describing the work and what the price depends on:

  • Radiator repair: bleeding, leaking valves, thermostatic heads.
  • Shower cabin repair and underfloor heating repair.
  • Concealed cistern repair and trap replacement.
  • Plumbing rough-in and sewer pipe replacement.
  • Bath to shower conversion, shower system installation, bidet installation, bath sealing.
  • Plumbing in a new-build apartment and plumbing installation in a private house.
  • Winterising a summer house water system.

If you are not sure which job you need, describe the problem in the form. We agree the price before the work.

Plumbing installation

Plumbing installation covers both fitting new fixtures and replacing old ones: toilets, sinks, baths, shower cabins, water heaters (boilers) and mixer taps, plus connecting household appliances. Each job has its own order: first the pipes and outlets, then the pressure test, then the fixture and its test with water.

If fixtures are replaced during a bathroom renovation, the plumber is needed twice: before tiling and after it. More detail: bathroom plumbing. We agree the price before the work.

Installation and replacement jobs that have their own pages:

  • Riser replacement: water and sewer risers and the pipes in the apartment
  • Toilet installation: floor-standing and wall-hung toilets
  • Bath replacement: removing the old bath and installing the new one
  • Radiator replacement: in apartments and private houses
  • Towel rail replacement: connection to the heating or hot water system
  • Heating system flushing: flushing sediment out of radiators and pipes
  • Plumbing prices: prices for all jobs in one place
