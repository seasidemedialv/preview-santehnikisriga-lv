About This Service

A dripping or broken faucet is not just an annoying sound - it also means significant water waste and potential flooding risks. Our plumbers carry out faucet repair and replacement quickly and professionally - usually within a single visit. We work with all types of faucets: single-lever, dual-handle, thermostatic, kitchen, bath and shower. We offer both repair and complete replacement - if the damage is beyond repair or the client wants a new model.

Bath faucet installation

A bath faucet is most often mounted on the wall above the bath. The wall connections are a set distance apart, and the eccentric connectors allow the width of the new faucet to be adjusted slightly. If the distance doesn't match or the connections are crooked, the eccentrics won't hold, and the outlets in the wall have to be rebuilt.

A deck-mounted faucet on the rim of the bath is fitted on acrylic baths, which are drilled for it, with the supply pipes run under the bath. It looks neater, but the connections have to be reached through a hatch in the bath panel, so this must be planned before the panel is tiled.

  • The shower hose and handset can be replaced without tools, but if it leaks at the diverter, the faucet is at fault.
  • The shower rail is fixed to the wall clear of the pipes: the pipe positions are found before drilling.

Faucet repair starts from €25, and we agree the replacement price before the work. If the bath needs replacing at the same time, see bath replacement. For a concealed thermostat, see shower system installation.

Kitchen faucet dripping or due for replacement

If the kitchen faucet drips from the spout, the cartridge is most often worn. If water leaks under the base of the faucet or into the cabinet, the fault is in a seal, the pull-out spray hose or the supply hoses. The hose of a pull-out head rubs inside the cabinet and splits, and it can be replaced on its own if the manufacturer offers one.

When fitting a new faucet with a pull-out head, we check that there is room in the cabinet for the hose weight and that it doesn't fall onto the trap. A sensor faucet needs space for the power supply or batteries, and its control unit has to be kept somewhere dry.

If the faucet is replaced along with the sink, it pays to do both jobs in one visit: sink installation. Faucet repair starts from €25, parts extra.

Replacing a faucet cartridge or tap valve

A single-lever faucet has a ceramic cartridge, while a two-handle faucet has a tap valve (headgear) on each side. Both are replaced the same way: shut off the water, remove the handle and the decorative cap, unscrew the old part and fit a new one with the same size and thread.

  1. 1

    Identifying the part

    For a cartridge, the diameter and the shape of the stem matter; for a tap valve, the thread and the number of splines. It is best to bring the old part along.

  2. 2

    Replacement

    The seat is cleaned of limescale, otherwise the new cartridge will drip too.

  3. 3

    Testing

    The water is turned on, and the hot and cold sides are checked, as well as whether anything leaks under the handle.

If a cartridge is no longer available for that faucet or the body is cracked, it is better value to replace the whole faucet. We agree the price before the work.

Water supply hose replacement

The flexible supply hoses under the sink, at the toilet cistern and at the water heater (boiler) are one of the most common causes of flooding. They are rubber hoses with a metal braid, and the rubber becomes brittle over time. If the braid is rusty, the hose is swollen or damp at the nut, it is worth replacing straight away.

When you replace a faucet, replace the supply hoses too, because the old ones are often as old as the faucet. The hose must be the right length: too short and it is stretched, too long and it kinks, and either way it fails sooner.

If a supply hose has already burst, close the shut-off valve or the inlet valve and see water leak repair. Leak repair starts from €45.

Faucet replacement

Replacing the faucet (mixer tap) is the right choice if the body is cracked, the tap still leaks at the base after the seals have been replaced, or spare parts are no longer available for that model. If only the cartridge or a seal is faulty, a repair is usually enough, and it costs less.

  1. 1

    Shutting off the water

    The shut-off valves under the sink are closed. If there are none or they don't hold, the water is shut off at the apartment inlet.

  2. 2

    Removing the old faucet

    The flexible supply hoses and the fixing are unscrewed. The old hoses are usually replaced along with the faucet, because the rubber becomes brittle over time.

  3. 3

    Fitting the new faucet

    The faucet is fixed in place and hot and cold water are connected on the correct sides. For a wall-mounted faucet the eccentric connectors are adjusted, because the standard distance between the connections is 150 mm.

  4. 4

    Testing

    The water is turned on and the connections are checked under pressure, along with the aerator and the diverter.

We can install a faucet you have bought yourself, or help you choose a model that fits the existing connections. If you are also replacing the sink, it pays to combine both jobs in one visit: sink installation.

Faucet types and the most common faults

Where a fault occurs depends on how the faucet is built. The table shows what fails most often and what is done about it.

Faucet typeMost common faultUsual fix
Single-leverWorn ceramic cartridge: drips or the temperature is hard to setCartridge replacement
Two-handleWorn washer or tap valve (headgear)Washer or tap valve replacement
ThermostaticLimescale in the thermostatic element, non-return valves not holdingCleaning or thermostatic element replacement
Kitchen faucet with pull-out sprayCracked hose, blocked aeratorHose or aerator replacement
Wall-mounted bath or shower faucetLeaking diverter or eccentric connector threadsDiverter repair, resealing the eccentrics

If the pressure is weak at just one tap, the aerator is checked first. If the pressure is weak throughout the apartment, the cause is usually elsewhere: low water pressure.

Concealed and thermostatic faucets

The body of a concealed (built-in) faucet sits inside the wall, and only the panel with the handle is visible. The body is set into the wall before tiling, so choosing the right depth matters: if it is too deep or too shallow, the decorative panel cannot be fitted properly.

For repairs, a concealed faucet is reached through the panel, and the wall usually doesn't need to be broken open. Spare parts (cartridge, diverter) are specific to the manufacturer, so it helps to know the brand and model. If you don't know them, describe the faucet in the form.

A thermostatic faucet holds the set temperature even when the pressure changes. In apartment blocks it has one typical problem: if the non-return valves don't hold, hot water can flow into the cold water pipe, and you notice it at other taps too. This is fixed by cleaning or replacing the non-return valves.

What the price of a faucet repair depends on

Faucet repair starts from €25. The final price depends on the scope of work, and we agree it before the work.

  • Whether a seal or aerator is enough, or the cartridge or the whole faucet has to be replaced
  • Whether a spare part is available for that model
  • Whether the nuts and eccentric connectors are rusted and hard to undo
  • Whether the shut-off valves and flexible hoses need replacing at the same time
  • Whether the faucet is on the sink, on the wall or concealed in the wall

Materials (the new faucet, spare parts) are usually charged separately from the labour.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly does the plumber arrive?

After you send the form we contact you within 5 minutes and agree a time. For emergencies we try to come as soon as we can.

How often should supply hoses be replaced?

There is no single interval; it depends on the manufacturer and the water. Check them when you replace a faucet, and replace them straight away if you see rust in the braid, swelling or damp at the nut.

Can just the cartridge be replaced instead of the whole faucet?

Yes, if the body is intact and a cartridge is available for that model. It is the most common repair on a single-lever faucet, and it usually costs less than buying a new faucet.

What if there are no shut-off valves under the sink?

Then the water is shut off at the apartment inlet. When replacing the faucet it is worth fitting angle shut-off valves too, so next time the faucet can be repaired without cutting off water to the whole apartment.
