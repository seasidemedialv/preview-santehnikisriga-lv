Shower tray replacement

A tray is replaced when it is cracked, water leaks under it, or an old high tray needs to be swapped for a low one. A low tray looks better and is more comfortable, but it leaves less room for the trap, so the height and fall of the drain outlet are checked first.

  • An acrylic tray needs a stable frame or legs, otherwise it flexes and the joint cracks.
  • A stone resin tray is heavy and rigid; it is laid on a level base or on mortar.
  • With a low tray the trap may not line up with the old outlet; sometimes the outlet has to be lowered into the floor.

After installation the tray is tested with water before silicone is applied along the wall. If instead of a tray you want a shower with a floor drain, or to replace the bath entirely, see bath to shower conversion. We agree the price before the work.

Linear drain replacement and repair

A linear floor drain is more likely to leak or smell if it was installed without proper waterproofing or with too little fall. The smell most often comes from a water seal that has dried out or was fitted wrongly, and it is fixed by cleaning the trap and putting it back correctly.

If water is leaking under the tiles, the problem is in the drain's joint with the waterproofing or with the sewer pipe. Then the tiles around the drain often have to be taken up. Before that we check whether the leak is inside the drain or at the joint by the wall, because a silicone joint is a much simpler repair.

If hair has built up in the drain and water runs away slowly, cleaning is enough. If the smell doesn't go away, see sewage smell. For cabin repairs, see shower cabin repair.

Glass shower screen installation

A walk-in glass shower screen or shower doors are a glazier's job, but they only work if the plumbing has been prepared correctly. We work on the base of the shower: the floor drain or tray, the floor slope, the mixer tap and the shower system.

  • The floor in the shower area must fall towards the drain, otherwise water runs under the glass onto the bathroom floor.
  • The position of the glass profile must be known before tiling, so the drain is not placed too close to it.
  • The mixer tap and shower head are positioned so the spray doesn't point straight at the opening.

If the glass screen is already in place and water gets out, the cause is often the floor slope or a damaged seal. For the shower mixer and head, see shower system installation. Shower cabin installation starts from €80.

Shower floor drain installation

A shower without a tray, with a drain in the floor, looks simple, but it needs more preparation than a shower cabin. The floor drain is installed before the floor is levelled and tiled, so it has to be agreed in good time.

  • Height: the drain with its water seal and the pipe to the riser need height in the floor; in apartment blocks this often means a raised floor in the shower area
  • Slope: the floor must slope towards the drain, usually 1–2%, otherwise water stays on the tiles
  • Type: a point drain goes in the middle or a corner of the shower, a linear drain along the wall; with a linear drain the floor needs only one slope, and it is easier to tile
  • Waterproofing: the edge of the drain must be joined to the waterproofing laid before the tiles; this work is planned together with the tiler
  • Cleaning: the drain must stay accessible, and the hair strainer and water seal need regular cleaning

If the drain smells, the water seal has often dried out: sewage smell. The price depends on the drain type and the floor build-up, and we agree it after an inspection.
