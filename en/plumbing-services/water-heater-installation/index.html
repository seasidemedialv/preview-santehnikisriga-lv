Water heater replacement: how it works

Replacing a water heater (boiler) is two jobs: removing the old one and installing the new one. The old tank is often full of limescale and heavy, so it is first drained through the cold water inlet.

  1. 1

    Shut-off and draining

    The power is switched off, the cold water shut off and the tank drained into the waste pipe.

  2. 2

    Removal

    The supply connections are disconnected and the water heater is taken off its brackets.

  3. 3

    Checking the mounting

    A new water heater often has a different bracket spacing. The wall is checked to make sure it can bear the weight of a full tank, and new fixings are drilled if needed.

  4. 4

    Connection

    A new safety valve, shut-off valves and flexible or rigid supply connections are fitted.

  5. 5

    Start-up

    The tank is filled, air is let out through a hot water tap, and only then is the power switched on and the joints checked.

If the new water heater is bigger than the old one, check that the electrical circuit and fuse suit its power; a new circuit is installed by an electrician. Water heater installation starts from €45; removal and disposal of the old unit are agreed separately. If the old water heater is still sound, consider a water heater repair.

How water heater cleaning works

  1. 1

    Shut-off

    The water heater's power is switched off and the water is left to cool.

  2. 2

    Draining

    The cold water is shut off, a hot water tap is opened and the tank is drained.

  3. 3

    Opening the flange

    The bottom cover is removed and the flange with the heating element and anode is taken out.

  4. 4

    Removing the sediment

    Limescale is cleared from the bottom of the tank, the heating element is cleaned and the anode is checked.

  5. 5

    Reassembly

    The flange is refitted, usually with a new gasket, the tank is filled with water and only then is the power switched on.

Water heater cleaning starts from €35. If during cleaning the anode turns out to be worn or the heating element damaged, they can be replaced during the same visit; we agree the price of parts before the work.

If the water still heats slowly after cleaning, the cause may be the heating element or the thermostat: water heater repair.

Connecting an electric water heater

Connecting an electric water heater has two parts: the water side and the electrical side. The plumber mounts the water heater and connects the water, but the electrical connection must match the water heater's power rating.

  • Mounting: a full 80-litre water heater weighs around 100 kg, so it is fixed to a load-bearing wall with suitable anchors
  • Cold water inlet: a shut-off valve, then a safety valve with a non-return valve; there must be no tap between the safety valve and the water heater
  • Safety valve drain: the valve needs an outlet to the waste pipe, because it drips while the water heats
  • Pressure reducer: if the mains water pressure is higher than the manufacturer's limit, a reducer is fitted before the water heater
  • Hot water outlet: connected to the apartment's hot water pipes or straight to a mixer tap
  • Electricity: the water heater is connected to an earthed socket or to a dedicated circuit with a breaker; a new circuit is installed by an electrician

Always fill the tank with water before switching on. A water heater switched on empty quickly ruins its heating element.

Water heater (boiler) cleaning and maintenance

Water heater cleaning is a different service from installing a new water heater: it is regular maintenance for an existing unit that is already in place. Over time, limescale and sediment build up in the tank, especially given the hardness of Riga's water. This reduces heating efficiency, increases electricity use and shortens the water heater's service life.

  • Cleaning limescale from the tank: we drain the water heater and clear the sediment from the heating element and the inside of the tank
  • Checking and cleaning the heating element: limescale on the heating element is the most common reason for slower water heating
  • Checking the anode rod: a worn anode speeds up tank corrosion, so we check it and replace it if needed
  • Checking the safety valve: we make sure the pressure relief valve is working correctly

We recommend cleaning a water heater once every 1–2 years, depending on the water hardness and how heavily the water heater is used. Regular maintenance costs less than replacing a water heater early. Water heater cleaning starts from €35.
