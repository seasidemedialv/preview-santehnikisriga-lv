Pipes that hum, bang or whistle

Noise in the pipes is a sign, not a fault in itself, but it is worth finding the cause. The sound and the moment it happens help you work out the cause.

What you hearMost common cause
A bang when a tap is closed quickly or a machine cuts off the waterWater hammer: high pressure, loose pipes
Humming or droning while a tap is openWorn mixer tap cartridge or valve head, a half-closed valve
WhistlingA narrowing: a clogged valve, limescale, a pressure reducer
Knocking along the riser at nightA neighbour's appliance or the building's system

If the banging keeps coming back, the pipes need fixing in place and the pressure needs checking: high pressure is lowered with a reducer, see low water pressure. If the noise comes from the riser, tell the house manager. Diagnostics and repair start from €35.

Sweating, damp pipes

If the cold water pipes or the toilet cistern are wet but nothing is leaking, it is condensation. The cold pipe carries water that is cooler than the air in the room, and moisture from the air settles on the pipe. This often happens in a toilet with no ventilation and in summer.

Condensation is not a leak, but the water running down the pipes damages the floor and the boxing over time. Insulation around the cold water pipes and better ventilation help. If only one joint is damp, even in dry weather, it is a leak and the joint needs replacing.

There is a simple test: wipe the pipe dry and wrap a paper napkin around the joint. If the napkin gets wet only at the joint, it is leaking.

Replacing old galvanised pipes

Galvanised steel pipes are still found in many pre-war and Soviet-era apartments. They rust from the inside, narrow the flow and start leaking at the joints. The LV portāls summary of Cabinet Regulation No. 906 gives a service life of 15–30 years for water pipes, depending on the type.

If one spot is leaking but the rest of the pipes are just as old, a repair is often only temporary. It is then better value to replace all the apartment's pipes from the inlet valve to the appliances, usually with polypropylene or multilayer pipes. Replacing the pipes in an apartment starts from €500 for the labour.

Old cast-iron drain pipes have their own page: sewer pipe replacement. If the riser is old as well, talk to the house manager: riser replacement.

Source: LV portāls, explanation of the service life of apartment buildings (Annex 2 to Cabinet Regulation No. 906). Checked 05.11.2026.

Pipe installation

Pipe installation is needed when water or drainage has to be run to a new place: moving the kitchen, connecting a washing machine in another room, rebuilding the bathroom or replacing the old steel pipes in an apartment.

  1. 1

    Planning the route

    We decide where the outlets will be, which way the pipes will run and where the shut-off valves will go so they stay accessible.

  2. 2

    Choosing the material

    Water supply usually uses polypropylene or multilayer pipes, and drainage uses plastic pipes with sealing rings.

  3. 3

    Installation

    Drain pipes are laid with a fall towards the riser (usually about 2%), and water pipes are given room for insulation.

  4. 4

    Pressure test

    Before the wall or floor is closed up, the system is pressure-tested so that a leak does not first appear under the tiles.

Planned replacement of all the pipes in an apartment together with the risers is described on the riser replacement page.

When a new pipe is joined to an old one, adapters between materials are used. During a repair it is worth finding out how much of the old steel pipework is still left, because the next leak often appears right next to the spot just fixed.

Riser or apartment pipe: who is responsible

In an apartment building the water and drain risers are common property. The pipes from the riser to the appliances belong to the apartment. If the riser itself or its joint is leaking, first inform the house manager, because the apartment owners decide on riser replacement and how it is paid for.

Pipes beyond the riser, inside the apartment, are repaired and replaced by the apartment owner. More detail: who pays for riser replacement.

Section 3 of the Apartment Property Law (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums): an apartment includes the utility networks up to the common risers; the risers are common property of the building, and the owners' meeting decides on their replacement (LV portāls, 24.10.2024). Checked 05.11.2026.

Why do the pipes bang when the washing machine takes in water?

The machine's valve closes quickly and the flow in the pipe stops with a jolt. If the pipes are loose or the pressure is high, you hear it. Fixing the pipes in place and checking the pressure helps.

Can pipes be replaced without breaking open the walls?

Partly. Exposed pipes and those running in ducts or behind panels can be replaced without much demolition. If a pipe is set in concrete or under tiles, it is often cheaper to run a new route another way than to chisel out the old one.

What should I do if a pipe has burst?

Shut off the water at the apartment inlet and, if the water is getting near electrics, switch off the power. If the riser is leaking, tell the house manager straight away. We contact you within 5 minutes of your request.

How long do water pipes last?

According to the table in Cabinet Regulation No. 906, cold water and drain pipes last 15–30 years depending on the type, and cast-iron drainage 60 years. In many of Riga's Soviet-era buildings this time has already passed.
