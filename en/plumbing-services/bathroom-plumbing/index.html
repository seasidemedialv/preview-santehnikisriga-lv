Why Choose Us?

The bathroom has more plumbing elements per square metre than any other room in a home. An incorrectly installed shower drain, careless pipe connections or poor sealing means moisture problems, mould and expensive repairs in the future. Services from €200.

Second-fix plumbing after tiling

Once the tiles are laid and the grout has dried, the second fix begins: all the fixtures are installed on the outlets prepared earlier. The usual order is:

  1. 1

    Toilet

    A wall-hung toilet is hung on the frame; a floor-standing toilet is levelled and connected.

  2. 2

    Bath or shower cabin

    It is installed, the trap is connected and it is tested with water.

  3. 3

    Sink with cabinet

    It is levelled, then the trap and mixer tap are connected.

  4. 4

    Mixer taps and shower system

    Concealed mixer trims, wall-mounted mixer taps and the shower rail are fitted.

  5. 5

    Silicone joints

    Last of all, once every fixture is in place and tested.

Tiles are drilled only with a tile drill bit and never close to the pipes in the wall, so a photo of the pipework taken before tiling is useful. If an outlet height does not suit the chosen fixture, it is better to know before tiling. Bathroom plumbing starts from €200. If you are fitting fixtures in a new apartment, see new-build plumbing.

Bathrooms in Soviet-series and pre-war buildings

In Riga's standard-series blocks (the 119, 467 and 602 series and others) and in pre-war buildings, the riser dictates the plumbing in a bathroom renovation. It usually runs through the bathroom or the toilet, and it determines where the toilet can go and how far the shower waste can run with enough fall.

The LV portāls summary of Cabinet Regulation No. 906 gives a service life of 15–30 years for water and sewer pipes and 60 years for cast-iron sewer pipes, and in many of these bathrooms the pipes and risers are original. If they are not replaced during the renovation, the new tiles will have to be broken open later. So ask the house manager about plans for the risers before you renovate.

If you want to combine the bathroom and the toilet, first read combining a bathroom and toilet, because this often affects common property too.

Source: LV portāls, explanation of the service life of apartment buildings (Annex 2 to Cabinet Regulation No. 906). Checked 05.11.2026.

The order of plumbing work in a bathroom renovation

A bathroom needs a plumber twice: before tiling, when the pipes and concealed parts go in, and after tiling, when the fixtures are installed. If the order is not followed, the new tiles later have to be drilled or the outlets rebuilt.

  1. 1

    Removal and inspection

    The old fixtures are removed, the pipes and riser connections inspected, and we decide what needs replacing.

  2. 2

    Planning the outlets

    Together with you we set the positions and heights of the toilet, sink, shower and washing machine.

  3. 3

    Pipes and frames

    The water supply and drainage are run, and the concealed toilet frame, concealed mixer tap and shower floor drain are installed.

  4. 4

    Pressure test

    The system is tested under pressure while the pipes are still visible.

  5. 5

    Fitting the fixtures

    After tiling, the toilet, sink, bath or shower cabin and mixer taps are installed, and then everything is checked together.

Risers and common property in the bathroom

The water and sewer risers usually run through the bathroom, often with the towel rail loop too. Risers are the building's common property, so they must stay accessible: if you box them in, leave an access hatch.

If during the renovation you see that the riser is old, talk to the house manager before tiling. The owner replaces the apartment's pipes up to the riser, but the apartment owners decide on replacing the risers: riser replacement. If the towel rail is connected to the building's heating system, agree its replacement with the house manager: radiator replacement approval.

Section 3 of the Apartment Property Law (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums): the apartment includes utility networks up to the common risers; the risers are common property, and the owners' meeting decides on replacing them (LV portāls, 24.10.2024). Checked 05.11.2026.

Most common bathroom plumbing mistakes

  • A sewer pipe without enough fall: water stands and a smell develops
  • No shut-off valves at the fixtures, so every repair means shutting off the water to the whole apartment
  • Pipes tiled over without a pressure test
  • The concealed mixer tap body set at the wrong depth
  • The riser connection boxed in without access
  • Not enough height for the shower floor drain, and the wrong floor slope

If a smell appears after the renovation, see sewage smell.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does bathroom plumbing installation in Riga cost?

Bathroom plumbing services in Riga start from €200. We confirm the exact price before starting work - no hidden fees.

How quickly does the plumber arrive?

After you send the form we contact you within 5 minutes and agree a time. For emergencies we try to come as soon as we can.

Can the fixtures be installed right after tiling?

After the grout has dried, as the grout manufacturer specifies. Silicone joints are done last, once all fixtures are installed and tested with water.

When should I book a plumber during a bathroom renovation?

The first time after removal, when the fixture layout is known and tiling has not started. The second time after tiling, when the fixtures are installed.

Can the risers be replaced together with the bathroom renovation?

Yes, if it is agreed with the house manager, because the riser is common property. It is the best moment, since the wall is already open.
