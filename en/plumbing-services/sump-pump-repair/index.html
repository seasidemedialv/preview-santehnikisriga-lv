Sump pump service - what it is

Many private houses, and some apartment building basements, have a sump (basement) pump installed to collect and move groundwater or seeped-in rainwater away from the building's foundations. This pump normally runs automatically via a float switch, and stays unnoticed most of the time - until the moment it stops working.

We offer sump pump diagnosis, repair, replacement and new system installation. This is a specific, relatively niche service - most often we come across it when a client already has a sump system installed and it stops working, or when a client wants a pump installed as a precaution after a previous water build-up in the basement.

Why accurate diagnosis matters

A sump pump is one of those things people only notice once it stops working - and by then there are often already visible signs of water in the basement. Our technician first checks the whole system - the float switch, motor, discharge pipe - to find the exact cause, rather than immediately proposing the most expensive fix. If the pump can be repaired at a reasonable cost, we do that; if it's completely worn out, we'll honestly recommend a replacement.

Sololift and macerator unit repair

A compact macerator unit behind the toilet, often called a sololift after one manufacturer's product name, lets you put a toilet in a basement or far from the riser. It has a macerator, a pump and a non-return valve, and any of these parts can fail.

Basement plumbing and a sewer backflow valve

If the basement has a shower, washing machine or toilet below the level of the street sewer, a heavy downpour or a blockage can make the sewage flow back and flood the basement through the floor drain. To prevent this, a sewer backflow valve is fitted: it lets waste water through in one direction only.

  • The backflow valve goes on the pipe from the basement appliances, not on the whole house drainage, so the upper floors can keep using water.
  • It must stay accessible: the valve is checked and cleaned from time to time, otherwise it may not close.
  • If the appliances are below the sewer at all times, what you need is not a valve but a sewage pump.

We give the price after an inspection, once the pipe diameter and location are known. If the basement has already flooded, see flooded apartment.

Sewage pump installation

A sewage pump is needed when a toilet, shower or washing machine sits lower than the drain pipe, for example in a basement, or so far from the riser that a proper fall cannot be maintained. It is a different pump from a sump pump: it pumps waste water, not groundwater.

TypeWhat it suits
Compact macerator unit behind the toiletOne toilet and a sink or shower in the basement
Pump in a tank under the floorSeveral appliances or a whole basement bathroom
Grey water pumpOnly a shower, sink or washing machine, no toilet
  • A non-return valve in the discharge pipe, so waste water does not flow back
  • Discharge pipe diameter and lift height as the manufacturer specifies
  • Ventilation for the tank, so the room does not smell
  • A dedicated socket with residual current protection; this is installed by an electrician
  • Access to the pump for servicing and cleaning

A sewage pump is the answer where gravity drainage is not possible; if drainage with a fall can be built, it is more reliable. We give the price after an inspection, once the pump type and discharge pipe length are known. If there is already a pump but the waste water will not go away, see drain cleaning.
