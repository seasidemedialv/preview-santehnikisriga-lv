Riser replacement in an apartment in Riga

Riser replacement in an apartment starts with the house manager, because the riser is common property of the whole building and replacing it means shutting off and draining the entire riser. We replace the water or drain riser section in your apartment once the work has been agreed, and at the same time we can also replace the pipes and valves after the inlet. Replacing one riser section costs from €100, and we agree the price before the work starts.

What is special about riser replacement

The Apartment Property Law (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums) draws the line at the riser: the pipes up to the common property risers belong to the apartment, while the risers themselves are part of the building's utility systems. That is why the owners' meeting decides on riser replacement and its funding, and the house manager organises the work.

In practice this means three things. The water is shut off for the whole riser, not just your apartment. The new section has to join the pipe on the neighbours' floors above and below, which often remains old cast iron or steel. And in many apartments the riser is boxed in or tiled over, so access has to be opened first.

Who pays for what, and what to do if the house manager is not planning a replacement, is explained in the article who pays for riser replacement.

Sources: Section 3, Paragraph 2 and Section 4, Paragraph 1 of the Apartment Property Law (likumi.lv); LV portāls e-consultation of 24.10.2024 on risers as common property. Checked 05.11.2026. This information is not legal advice for your building's situation.

How riser replacement in an apartment works

  1. 1

    Approval from the house manager

    The house manager approves the work, the materials and the day the riser will be shut off. If the whole building is organising the replacement, we follow the procedure set by the house manager.

  2. 2

    Access

    We look at where the riser runs through the apartment and whether it is covered by boxing or tiles. Removing and restoring the boxing or tiles is agreed in advance.

  3. 3

    Shut-off and draining

    The riser is shut off and drained by the house manager or their service company. The neighbours on the same riser are warned about the water interruption.

  4. 4

    Removing the old section

    The old cast iron, steel or galvanised section is cut out within the floor, and the ends are prepared for joining to the pipe on the neighbours' floors.

  5. 5

    New section and connections

    The new riser section is installed with transitions to the existing pipe, a branch for the apartment and, if agreed, new valves.

  6. 6

    Filling and testing

    With the house manager's permission the riser is refilled, and the joints are checked for leaks at every connection point.

Pipe replacement in the apartment together with the riser

The pipes after the inlet valve up to the taps, toilet and bath are your apartment's property, and you can order their replacement yourself. It is best value to do this when the riser is shut off anyway: the old pipes are without water, and the new system starts from a new branch.

Usually the water and drain connections in the bathroom and the branch to the kitchen are replaced. The pipes can be run exposed or concealed in the wall, in plastic (PPR), cross-linked polyethylene (PEX) or multilayer pipe. Concealed installation needs wall chasing and restoration of the finish, which is not plumbing work.

If only one joint is leaking and the pipes are in good condition overall, pipe repair is enough without replacing the whole system.

Valve replacement: where the building's responsibility ends

In 2023 the Senate (Supreme Court) ruled that the water inlet valve fitted where the supply enters the apartment is part of the building's common water supply system. If the inlet valve leaks or does not close, tell the house manager: replacing it means shutting off the riser, and it is a matter for the building.

The valves after the inlet are up to you: the shut-off valves at the cistern, the sink and the washing machine, and the flexible connectors. It is worth replacing them together with the pipes, because old ball valves that have not been moved for years often no longer close fully.

Source: Senate judgment of 21.09.2023 in case SKC-535/2023 (at.gov.lv). Checked 05.11.2026.

Risers in 119, 602 and 467 series buildings

In the LV portāls summary of Cabinet Regulation No. 906, buildings of the 119 series are given a service life of 60 years, and 467 and 602 series buildings 70 years. For water and drain pipes this period is much shorter: 15–30 years depending on the type, and 60 years for cast iron drainage with cast iron fittings.

In the districts where these series dominate, the buildings went up from the 1960s to the 1980s. Construction in Zolitūde began in 1983, and the 119 series dominates there; in Pļavnieki 602 and 119 series buildings were built from 1985; in Mežciems the 602 series was built in 1975–1986; in Imanta the 464, 467 and 602 series dominate. If the water pipes and risers have not been replaced since construction, they have already exceeded their standard service life.

More about plumbing in these districts: in Zolitūde, in Pļavnieki, in Mežciems, in Imanta and in Purvciems.

Sources: LV portāls, explainer on the service life of apartment buildings (Annex 2 to Cabinet Regulation No. 906); apkaimes.lv history pages (Zolitūde, Pļavnieki, Mežciems, Imanta). Checked 05.11.2026.
