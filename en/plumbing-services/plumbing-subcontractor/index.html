A plumbing partner for your renovation projects

If you run a renovation or small construction company, one of the most common headaches is finding a plumber who sticks to the project schedule and doesn't hold up the other tradespeople. We work as a plumbing subcontractor on apartment and house renovation projects - from pipework installation at the start of a renovation to final fixture connection at the end of the job.

Our focus is residential-scale projects - apartment and house renovations, not commercial or industrial sites. Before work begins we agree the scope, schedule and price, and after completion we issue a clear invoice.

What plumbing subcontracting includes

From rough-in work to final fixture connection

Pipework installation (rough-in)

Water supply and drainage system installation at the start of a renovation

Fixture connection (finish work)

Connecting toilets, sinks, baths and showers at the end of the job

Mixer tap & appliance installation

Precise connection according to the renovation plan's specification

Water heater & heating connections

Installing water heaters and heating elements as part of the renovation

Pressure testing before handover

Testing the system before the space is finished and handed over to the client

Schedule coordination

We agree a specific time slot that fits your project

Why a reliable plumbing partner matters on a renovation project

On a renovation project, every tradesperson works within their own time slot - if the plumber is late or doesn't show up when agreed, it can delay the tiler, the electrician, and ultimately the whole project timeline. That's why our priority is sticking to the agreed schedule and communicating promptly if anything changes. We work at apartment and house renovation scale and provide a clear invoice for completed work.
