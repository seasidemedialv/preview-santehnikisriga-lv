About This Service

Sink installation may seem straightforward, but improper fitting causes leaks, water damage, and costly repairs. Our plumbers ensure correct sink connection to both water supply and drain - with proper seals, traps and a leak test.

We install all types: kitchen sink installation (inset or undermount), bathroom sink installation (pedestal, vanity unit, wall-hung, countertop). If you have your own sink, we'll fit it. If you need help choosing one, say so in the form.

Replacing a basin and vanity unit in the bathroom

In the bathroom it is usually not just the basin that gets replaced, but the basin together with its vanity unit. The unit has a cut-out for the trap, so we first check whether the existing water and drain outlets will fit inside it. If the outlets are too high or off to the side, an opening is cut in the back of the unit or the outlets are moved.

  • The unit is levelled and fixed to the wall so the basin doesn't rock.
  • A double basin needs two traps with a shared branch or two outlets in the wall.
  • If the basin is loose or leaks at the wall, the cause is usually the fixings or the silicone joint, not the basin itself.

An old trap is worth replacing together with the basin: trap replacement. Sink installation starts from €35; assembling the vanity unit is agreed separately.

Fitting a kitchen sink into the worktop

For an inset kitchen sink, an opening is cut in the worktop using the manufacturer's template. The cut edge is sealed so moisture doesn't soak into the chipboard and make it swell. The sink is set on its gasket, fixed with the clips from the kit, and then the trap and mixer tap are connected.

If you are replacing the old kitchen sink with one of a different size, the new one must be at least as large as the old cut-out, otherwise the opening will remain visible. The position of the sink in the cabinet is chosen so there is room underneath for the trap, the shut-off valves and, if needed, the dishwasher supply. The worktop cut-out can be made by the kitchen fitter or by us; we agree this before the work.

Undermount and stone sink installation

An undermount sink is fixed to the worktop from below, so it only suits stone, quartz or compact laminate worktops, where the edge of the opening is polished and water doesn't soak in. The opening is usually cut and polished by the worktop manufacturer before the worktop is installed.

A stone resin sink is heavy, and heavier still when full of water. It needs a sturdy cabinet, and in the undermount version the sink is supported with the brackets and adhesive the manufacturer specifies. Silicone between the sink and the worktop is only a seal, not the load-bearing fixing.

Holes for the mixer tap and trap are not drilled in a stone sink with an ordinary drill: use a sink that already has the hole, or mount the tap in the worktop. We agree the price before the work, because it depends on the worktop and the type of fixing.

Plumbing for a new kitchen and the dishwasher branch

A new kitchen needs a plumber twice. The first time is before the cabinets are installed, when the water and drain outlets are checked or moved to match the kitchen plan. The second time is when the worktop is in place and the sink, mixer tap and appliances are installed.

  • Under the sink you need angle shut-off valves for hot and cold water, plus a separate valve for the dishwasher.
  • The dishwasher outlet is connected to the trap through a branch. The hose is run in a loop higher than the trap, so dirty water doesn't flow back into the machine.
  • The drain pipe from the sink to the riser needs a fall; if the kitchen is moved further away, this is planned in advance.

Connecting appliances is covered on the page washing machine and dishwasher connection, from €25. If outlets need to be moved, see plumbing rough-in.

Food waste disposer installation

A food waste disposer is installed under the kitchen sink, between the sink outlet and the trap. The sink's waste opening must fit the disposer mounting, so this is checked before you buy. Under the sink you need room for the unit and a socket with residual current protection; that is fitted by an electrician.

  • The disposer is connected to the trap with a rigid pipe, and if there is a dishwasher, its outlet goes to the disposer's branch.
  • In an old building with a shallow fall and furred-up pipes, ground waste can build up in the pipe, so the drain is checked all the way to the riser first.
  • Even with a disposer, don't pour fat or oil down the sink.

We agree the price before the work. If the kitchen drain is already slow, clean it first: drain not draining.

Sink height

The height of the sink has to be decided before the water and drain outlets are installed. There is no single required figure; the table shows the usual heights from the floor to the rim of the sink.

Sink typeUsual heightNotes
Wall-hung or pedestal bathroom basin80–85 cmTaller people find about 90 cm more comfortable
Basin on a vanity unit85–90 cmMeasured together with the unit
Countertop basin (bowl on top)85–90 cm to the rim of the bowlThe countertop must be lower than the bowl height
Kitchen sink85–90 cmMatches the worktop height
Children's basin55–70 cmDepends on the child's age

The drain outlet must be lower than the trap outlet so water runs away with a fall. If the new sink sits higher or lower than the old one, the water outlets may also have to be moved. That is why the height is chosen together with the trap and mixer tap, not after tiling.

If the trap leaks or water drains slowly, the trap can be replaced during the same visit: trap installation starts from €25. If the sink is blocked, see drain not draining.
