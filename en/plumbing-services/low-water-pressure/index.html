Most common causes of low water pressure

Our plumbers diagnose the exact cause first, rather than jumping to the most expensive fix

Clogged aerator

Limescale build-up at the tap end - the most common and cheapest cause to fix

Worn mixer tap cartridge

The internal mechanism wears out and restricts flow

Old, corroded pipes

A common issue in Soviet-era buildings with old metal pipes

Pressure reducer problem

A faulty or misadjusted valve lowers pressure throughout the apartment

Partly closed shut-off valve

Often left over after renovation work when a valve wasn't fully reopened

Building-wide pressure shortage

Sometimes linked to the centralised water supply, not your apartment

Why accurate diagnosis matters

Low water pressure isn't the same problem in every case, so the fix can't be the same either. If you try replacing the mixer tap yourself, but the real cause is a corroded pipe behind the wall, the problem won't go away - you'll just lose money and time. And the reverse: if you call in expensive pipe repair work when the actual culprit was only a clogged aerator, you'll overpay for unnecessary work. Our plumber first checks whether the problem is in one fixed spot or the whole apartment, and only then offers a precise, proportionate fix with a clear price.

Water pressure reducer: adjustment and replacement

A pressure reducer (or regulator) is a valve on the water inlet that lowers the pressure and keeps it steady downstream. In an apartment it is often right after the inlet valve, in a private house at the water inlet. If it is set too low, furred up or its internal strainer is clogged, the pressure drops at every tap at once.

  1. 1

    Check

    A pressure gauge is fitted to or read at the reducer, and the pressure before and after it is compared.

  2. 2

    Cleaning

    If the reducer has a strainer, it is removed and cleaned of rust and sand.

  3. 3

    Adjustment

    The adjusting screw is used to set a pressure that suits the water heater (boiler) and appliances according to the manufacturer's instructions.

  4. 4

    Replacement

    If the diaphragm or valve is damaged and the pressure fluctuates, the reducer is replaced.

Signs of excessive pressure are a constantly dripping water heater safety valve and pipes that bang when a tap is closed: pipes humming or knocking. If the reducer is at the building's inlet, the house manager adjusts it. Diagnosis starts from €25.

Water pressure booster pump

A water pressure booster pump raises the pressure in an apartment or house when the cause is low incoming pressure, not a blockage. That is why diagnosis comes first: a pump does not help against limescale in the aerator or furred-up pipes.

SolutionSuitable forWhat to consider
Compact in-line pumpOne appliance: shower, water heater or washing machineSwitches on with flow and can be heard while running
Booster set with pressure tankThe whole apartment or a private houseNeeds space, power and access for servicing
Adjusting or replacing the pressure reducerWhen the reducer lowers the pressure too muchOften the cheapest solution

In an apartment building the pump draws water from the common riser. If the pressure is low throughout the building or on the upper floors, that is a matter for the house manager, because management includes providing the water supply and maintaining the utilities. A powerful pump in one apartment can lower the pressure for the neighbours even further.

Section 6(2) of the Residential Property Management Law (Dzīvojamo māju pārvaldīšanas likums): mandatory management tasks include providing water supply services and the inspection, technical maintenance and routine repair of the building's utilities. Checked 05.11.2026.

Diagnosis starts from €25. We give the price for installing a pump after diagnosis, once we know which pump is needed.
