A broken boiler or water heater is one of the most frustrating plumbing issues - especially in cold months. Replacing the water heater's safety valve and thermostat

The safety valve sits on the water heater's cold water inlet and lets water out when the pressure in the tank gets too high. If the water heater (boiler) drips from the valve even when it is not heating, or the valve no longer lets water out when you turn its lever, it is replaced. The new valve must have the same opening pressure as the one the water heater manufacturer specifies.

  1. 1

    Shut-off

    The power is switched off and the cold water to the water heater is closed.

  2. 2

    Releasing the pressure

    A hot water tap is opened so there is no pressure in the tank. The water heater usually does not need to be fully drained.

  3. 3

    Replacement

    The old valve is unscrewed, the new one is fitted with a seal and its drain hose is connected to the waste pipe.

  4. 4

    Check

    The tank is refilled, the heating is switched on, and we check that the valve works and nothing leaks.

To replace the thermostat the water heater is drained, because the sensor sits in the tank next to the heating element. If the water heater gets too hot or does not switch off, the thermostat is often to blame. Water heater repair starts from €45, parts extra.

Hot water smells of sulphur

If only the hot water smells and the cold water does not, the cause is almost always in the water heater. In a tank where water stays warm for a long time, bacteria can multiply, and their reaction with a worn magnesium anode produces a hydrogen sulphide smell like rotten eggs.

  • The magnesium anode is checked and replaced if it is worn.
  • The tank is drained and cleaned of the sediment where bacteria live.
  • After cleaning, the water heater is heated once to a higher temperature, if the manufacturer allows it.

If the water heater has stood unused for a long time, for example at a summer house, drain and replace the water in the tank before use. If the cold water smells too, the cause is not the water heater but the water itself or the pipes. Water heater cleaning starts from €35: water heater installation and cleaning.

A little water from the safety valve while heating is normal, because water expands as it warms up. If the valve also drips when the water heater is not heating, it needs checking.

Repair or replace the water heater

A repair makes sense when the tank is sound: the heating element, thermostat, gaskets, anode and valves can all be replaced. If the tank itself is leaking, it cannot be fixed, and the answer is a new water heater.

The decision also depends on the age of the water heater and on whether spare parts for that model are still available. Diagnostics start from €25 and are included in the repair price; afterwards you know whether a repair is worth it. Heating element replacement starts from €45, thermostat repair from €35 and leak repair from €40. If you choose a new water heater, see water heater installation.

What to do if the water heater is leaking

  1. 1

    Switch off the power

    Pull out the plug or switch off the water heater's breaker in the fuse box.

  2. 2

    Shut off the cold water

    Close the shut-off valve on the water heater's cold water inlet. If there is none, shut off the water to the apartment.

  3. 3

    Do not drain the water heater while it is hot

    The water in the tank can be very hot. Draining is part of the repair and is done once the water has cooled.

  4. 4

    Fill in the form

    We contact you within 5 minutes of your request. If the water has reached the neighbours, see water leak repair.
