Common causes of well pump failure

For houses without a centralised water supply, the well or borehole pump is the foundation of the whole home's water system. When it stops, everything stops - from the shower to the washing machine.

1

No water pressure at all

Often points to a fault in the pump motor or the power supply

2

Pump runs non-stop

Most often a damaged pressure tank membrane is to blame

3

Pump short-cycles

Quickly shortens the pump's lifespan, best fixed as soon as possible

Why accurate diagnosis matters

Well pump problems often look identical from the outside, but the causes can be completely different - the power supply, the pump motor, the pressure tank, or the intake pipe. If you replace the pump when only the pressure tank membrane was actually at fault, you'll overpay and the problem may return. Our technician first checks the whole system - from power to pressure - and only then offers a proportionate solution with a clear price.
