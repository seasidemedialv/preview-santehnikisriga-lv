What to do immediately

The right actions in the first few minutes of a water leak can significantly reduce damage to your property.

1

Shut off the water

Find the main water shut-off valve in your apartment or basement and close it. This stops the leak immediately.

2

Book a plumber

Fill in the form - we contact you within 5 minutes and agree a time when a plumber can come.

3

Document the damage

Photograph the flooded area - this helps with insurance claims or compensation from neighbours.

Important: If water is near electrical appliances or your fuse box - do not attempt to fix the leak yourself. Call an electrician first, then the plumber.

Why a leak must be fixed immediately

Even a small water leak can turn into an expensive problem. Moisture spreads quickly through wall and floor structures, causing mould and structural damage that costs far more to repair than the original leak. Leaks from neighbours above can lead to legal claims and insurance disputes. Near electrical equipment, water creates a fire hazard. Our plumber fixes the damage and provides written documentation - which can be used for an insurance claim.

Water leak sensor

A water leak sensor is a small detector on the floor that reacts to water. The simplest sensors sound an alarm. Systems with motorised valves shut off the water to the apartment by themselves, even when nobody is at home.

  • Where to put sensors: under the washing machine and dishwasher, by the water heater (boiler), in the sink cabinet, by the toilet and by the riser access hatch
  • Where the valves go: on the cold and hot water pipes right after the apartment's inlet valves, so the whole apartment can be shut off
  • Power: systems with valves need a socket or batteries, and the batteries must be changed in time
  • Testing: check the sensor now and then by wetting its contacts, and close and open the valves so they do not seize

A sensor does not prevent a leak from the neighbours upstairs and does not help if a pipe bursts before the valves. It reduces damage from your own apartment's appliances and pipes.

The inlet valve where the supply enters the apartment is part of the building's common water supply system, so it is not replaced without the house manager knowing. Automatic valves are fitted after it, on the apartment's pipes. We agree the price before the work.

Senate judgment of 21.09.2023 in case SKC-535/2023: the water inlet valve installed where the supply enters the apartment is part of the building's common water supply system. Checked 05.11.2026.
