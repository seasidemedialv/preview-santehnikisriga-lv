What a pre-sale plumbing inspection is

Before selling a home, many owners want to know the condition of the plumbing - whether there are any visible leaks, rust or signs of wear that could raise questions during a buyer's viewing. We offer a visual plumbing inspection: we look at the visible pipework, mixer taps, traps, toilets and water heater, and give you a written assessment of their condition.

It's important to understand - this is not a formal or certified property inspection and it is not a legal requirement in the selling process. It's a practical, plumbing-specific check whose purpose is to give you clarity on the visible plumbing condition, so you can choose to fix small things before listing or before buyer viewings.

Why an honest assessment helps the selling process

No seller wants surprises during a buyer's viewing, or after the deal is done. Our approach is simple - we look at what can be seen, and tell you honestly what's fine and what might raise questions. We don't claim more than what's visible - we don't inspect hidden pipework and we don't provide a formal certificate, since that isn't our area of competence. But an honest assessment of the visible plumbing is often exactly what's needed to feel confident about the next steps in selling your home.

Plumbing inspection before buying an apartment

For a buyer, a plumbing survey shows how much money the pipes will need after the purchase, not just a cosmetic refresh. In the inspection we look at what is often missed at a viewing:

  • risers: material, rust, whether they have been replaced and whether the building plans to replace them;
  • pipes in the apartment: old galvanised or already plastic, joints and signs of leaks;
  • inlet valves and shut-off valves: whether they close;
  • drainage: whether every fixture drains, and whether there is any smell or damp under the bath;
  • the water heater (boiler) and its safety valve, and the water pressure at the taps.

The LV portāls summary of Cabinet Regulation No. 906 gives a service life of 15–30 years for water and sewer pipes and 60 years for cast-iron sewer pipes, so this matters in Soviet-era buildings. Ask the house manager about the condition of the risers too. After the inspection you receive a written assessment with recommendations. The inspection starts from €60.

Source: LV portāls, explanation of the service life of apartment buildings (Annex 2 to Cabinet Regulation No. 906). Checked 05.11.2026.
