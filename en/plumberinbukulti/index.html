Pressure sewerage for more than 100 households

Historically, part of Riga's groundwater supply system, the Baltezers pumping station complex, was built on the grounds of Bukulti manor. Now, for the Bukulti houses themselves, Rīgas ūdens plans to rebuild the water connections in the private-house area between the Jugla canal and the railway and to create pressure sewerage for more than 100 households. Construction is planned for 2026–2027.

In pressure sewerage, waste water from the house does not flow to the street pipe by gravity. Each user has their own small pumping station that pumps the waste water into a pressure main. Rīgas ūdens chose this solution because it can serve houses in a potential flood zone without letting flood water into the network.

Rebuilding the water connections means the start of the house inlet will change too. After the relaying, it is worth checking that the pressure in the house has not dropped and that sediment has not built up in the screens; low water pressure diagnosis starts from €25.

Network scope, timing and the reason for choosing pressure sewerage: Rīgas ūdens, “Development of the Bukulti water supply networks”. History of the manor waterworks: Rīgas ūdens history page. Checked 05.11.2026.

What the house pumping station will change

For the internal house sewerage this means a few things that matter less with a gravity connection.

  • The pump runs on electricity. During a longer power cut the station tank fills up, so it is worth cutting down on water use in the house at such times.
  • The pump copes badly with rubbish: wet wipes, hygiene products and fat block it much faster than an ordinary pipe.
  • The house outlet must reach the station exactly at the position and height set in the design.

Publicly available sources do not yet describe what will be the owner's responsibility after construction, such as servicing the station and its electricity supply. Find this out in the Rīgas ūdens technical conditions or the contract.

The ownership boundary and the house inlet

Rīgas ūdens is responsible up to the so-called ownership boundary. Usually this is the outer wall of the metering chamber; where there is no chamber, the boundary is the red line of the street, that is, the edge of the plot. Everything beyond it, including leaks, is maintained and repaired by the owner at their own cost.

In Bukulti, where the water connections will also be rebuilt, it is worth checking the inlet from the boundary to the house now. If the old pipe has leaked or freezes, it is better value to replace it together with the new connection rather than separately.

Ownership boundary: the Rīgas ūdens page on responsibility boundaries for water services. Checked 05.11.2026.

Until the connection: septic tank declaration and servicing

As long as the house has a septic tank or holding tank, Riga Binding Regulation No. 66 applies to it. By 1 May each year the owner submits a declaration of the volume of waste water removed, the septic tank must be serviced at the intervals set by the manufacturer, and if treated waste water is discharged into the environment, tests in an accredited laboratory are needed once every two years.

If the connection for your street is planned for 2027, it is worth weighing up beforehand whether a damaged septic tank is worth repairing. Internal work that will also be useful after the connection, such as sorting out the outlet and pipes, can be done now.

Declaration, servicing and tests: Riga Binding Regulation No. 66 (likumi.lv). Checked 05.11.2026.

The flood zone by Ķīšezers, the Jugla river and the canal

Along the shores of Ķīšezers, the Jugla river and the Jugla canal the flood risk exceeds 5–10% over large areas, and this also applies to some of the existing buildings. In the western and south-western part around the water bodies, groundwater is closer to the surface than 1.5 m, the ground is weak and the area waterlogged. Of the five detached-house plots, only one is built up (Bukulti, including Kanāla iela); the others are earmarked for development if the flood risk is removed.

  • A sump pump in the basement must have a free outlet that does not freeze; sump pump repair from €75.
  • If flood water can reach the house, it is better to place the water heater (boiler) and pumps above floor level.
  • After a flood, check that surface water has not got into the septic tank or well before using them again.
