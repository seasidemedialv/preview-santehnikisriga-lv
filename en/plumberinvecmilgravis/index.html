Plumber in Vecmīlgrāvis, Riga

A plumber in Vecmīlgrāvis works mainly in blocks of apartments: the residential areas here are almost entirely taken up by multi-storey buildings, built from the 1950s to the housing estates of the 1970s. The district lies in the farthest part of the city water network, and that affects the water in your tap. Here we cover this and other plumbing questions in Vecmīlgrāvis.

From 1950s blocks to 1970s housing estates

In Vecmīlgrāvis multi-storey buildings began to be built in the 1950s on the site where the old wooden houses were burnt down, and in the mid-1970s construction of housing estates began. Industrial areas take up 27.1% of the district and residential areas 13.3%, almost all of them blocks of apartments. Only 4.7 ha around Sniedzes iela is set aside for detached houses.

In the masonry blocks of the 1950s and the buildings of the 1970s the pipes vary in age, but if they have not been replaced, they are more than 50 years old. In such buildings you should expect rusted steel joints, cracks in old sewer fittings and valves that no longer close.

Before a bathroom renovation, ask the house manager whether riser replacement is planned for the building. If it is, it is worth timing your renovation to match, otherwise the new tiling around the riser will have to be opened up later.

The far end of the network: when the tap water is yellowish

RTU measurements from 2021 show that the risk of cloudy water is also higher in the more remote districts: Bolderāja, Daugavgrīva and Vecmīlgrāvis. In an apartment it is most often noticed as yellowish water in the morning or after work on the city network.

  • If only the hot water is cloudy, the cause is often the building's hot water system or the water heater (boiler), not the city network.
  • If the cold water is cloudy throughout the building, let it run and, if it does not clear, report it to the house manager.
  • Sediment clogs aerators, shower heads and the washing machine strainer; if the pressure has dropped only in your apartment, low water pressure diagnostics cost from €25.
Risk of cloudy water in remote districts, including Vecmīlgrāvis: RTU measurements from 2021, LV portāls publication. Checked 05.11.2026.

The apartment and the building: where the boundary lies

Under the Apartment Property Law (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), an apartment includes the utility lines up to the common property risers, while systems serving the whole building are common property. In Vecmīlgrāvis blocks this means the supply pipes from the riser to your taps, toilet and bath belong to the apartment, but the risers and basement mains belong to the whole building.

If a joint leaks after the apartment valve, we can repair it. If a riser or a basement pipe is leaking, report it to the house manager first; if the building plans to replace its risers, it is worth replacing the apartment supply pipes at the same time.

Boundary between the apartment and common property: Sections 3 and 4 of the Apartment Property Law (likumi.lv). Checked 05.11.2026.

The heating plant on Atlantijas iela, radiators and towel rails

In the north of the district, by Atlantijas iela, is the Vecmīlgrāvis heating plant. Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks manages several thousand buildings with district heating, but it is not publicly stated what heating each building in Vecmīlgrāvis has.

If the building has district heating, the radiator is connected to the whole building's system, so replacing it requires the house manager's approval and draining the riser; how to prepare for this is explained in the article radiator replacement approval. A towel rail is usually on the hot water riser, and it too needs the riser shut off. Radiator replacement starts from €70, towel rail replacement from €80.

Heating plant: apkaimes.lv. District heating in Riga and Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks buildings: LSM, 2022. Checked 05.11.2026.

Rīnūži, Mīlgrāvis and the Mangaļsala networks

In the northern part (Rīnūži) and near the Daugava the groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m and the soils are weak, while by the Mīlgrāvis canal there are up to 5 m of man-made deposits. In the centre and south of the district the groundwater lies at 1.5–3 m and deeper.

For a block of apartments in the shallow groundwater zone this means a damper basement, where the water mains and sewer outlets run, and the pipes rust faster there. For detached houses around Sniedzes iela, basement and inlet work is the owners' own responsibility; if water collects in the basement, a sump pump helps (repair from €75).

Rīgas ūdens plans to connect the new Mangaļsala networks to the trunk networks on Atlantijas iela in Vecmīlgrāvis. If you live near Atlantijas iela, follow Rīgas ūdens announcements about work on the network.
