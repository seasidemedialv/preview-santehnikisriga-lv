The old wooden housing

Historically, only small wooden buildings were allowed outside Riga, and in Sarkandaugava some of this wooden housing has survived. Large former industrial areas have been given mixed-use status in the city plan.

In a wooden house the plumbing was almost always installed later than the building itself, and often in several stages. The pipes run through wooden floors and walls, and a leak inside a floor can stay hidden for a long time until a stain appears on the ceiling or the floor starts to go mouldy. A wooden structure also moves with the moisture, so a rigidly fixed pipe can work loose at a joint over time.

Before buying an old wooden house or apartment, it is worth checking the condition of the pipes, the drainage and any hidden leaks. A pre-sale plumbing inspection starts from €60.

A wooden floor also changes a toilet replacement. If the wood under the old toilet is damp or rotten, the new toilet will not stand firmly on it, and the outlet seal will soon come away. The base must be restored first, then the toilet fitted. Toilet installation and replacement starts from €60, and an uneven floor or an outlet adapter raises the price.

The southern part, Daudersala and the banks: shallow groundwater

In the southern part of the district (the former Daugava valley), along the shore, on Daudersala and by the Mīlgrāvis, conditions are unfavourable and the groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m. The theoretical flood risk is up to 2%, but during big surges from the sea the low banks of Sarkandaugava flood heavily.

  • Water can rise into the basement from the ground; then you need a sump pit with a pump. Sump pump repair starts from €75.
  • During surges the city and yard sewers can be full, and waste water looks for a way back through the lowest sink or floor drain in the basement. In such a place it is worth considering a non-return valve on the outlet.
  • After a basement flood the outlet and floor drain often need cleaning, and it is worth checking for smells: smell diagnostics from €35.

If the house still has a local sewage system

In 2023 LSM reported that almost 10,000 households in Riga still deal with their waste water locally. Around 7,000 of them are waiting for the network to come closer to their home, while around 3,000 could connect but have not done so. Whether there is a sewer in the street for your Sarkandaugava house can be checked with Rīgas ūdens by address.

If there is a network in the street, connection to the city sewer is co-financed by the municipality (Riga Binding Regulation No. RD-23-207-sn, in force since July 2023). It applies to all districts except those of the phase 4 and 5 projects, and the application must be submitted within 3 years of completing the connection.

Inside a wooden house, several drains often need to be collected into one outlet with a correct fall, rather than simply attaching the pipe to the old septic tank line. We set the price for this work after an inspection and agree it before the work starts.

Extent of local sewage systems: LSM, 01.11.2023. Co-financing: Riga Binding Regulation No. RD-23-207-sn (Rīgas ūdens explanation). Checked 05.11.2026.

Winter in an old wooden house

If the pipes in a wooden house run along an outer wall or in an unheated crawl space, they are the first to freeze in a hard frost. If water stops running from one tap while others still run, that is the section that has frozen. Do not thaw it with an open flame in a wooden structure; thawing frozen pipes costs from €55.

If a section is not used in winter, for example a tap in the basement or one for the garden, it is worth fitting it with its own stop valve and a drain point. Then before the frost it can simply be shut off and drained.
