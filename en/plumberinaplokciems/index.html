Plumber in Aplokciems, Riga

A plumber in Aplokciems works in an area of detached houses in the eastern part of Mīlgrāvis, where the land is low and groundwater is close to the surface. Here plumbing is not just taps and pipes inside the house: pumps, the outside sewer and where the waste water ends up matter just as much. Below is a summary of what to keep in mind in Aplokciems.

Aplokciems in the east of the Mīlgrāvis district

Aplokciems is not a separate official district but the eastern part of the Mīlgrāvis district. In the north there is a golf club, and the rest is residential. The Riga spatial plan designates Aplokciems as an area for detached houses, but historically two-storey apartment houses also appeared here.

In a detached house everything from the street to the last tap is the owner's responsibility. In a two-storey house with several apartments, the shared pipes and the inlet belong to all the owners together, so larger jobs are agreed among them or with the house manager.

Low ground and storm surges from Ķīšezers

In the central, northern and north-eastern parts of the Mīlgrāvis district, building conditions are difficult and the groundwater level is high. In Aplokciems the land is low, and in places the flood risk exceeds 2%. During storm surges the maximum water level in Mīlgrāvis and Ķīšezers can exceed 2 m above sea level.

  • A pump in the basement or plant room must be automatic: with a float switch that turns it on as the water rises, not only when someone notices.
  • The pump outlet should point away from the house and not into the same ditch that fills first during a storm surge.
  • After a period of high water, check that the pump still switches on and that the outlet pipe is not clogged with sand.
  • If the pump hums but does not pump, sump pump repair costs from €75.
Groundwater, flood risk and storm surge levels: apkaimes.lv, Mīlgrāvis geography. Checked 05.11.2026.

Is there city water and sewerage in your street?

There is no public description of which streets in Aplokciems have mains networks and which do not. So the only reliable way is to ask Rīgas ūdens about your specific address.

If the network is available, a new connection requires technical conditions from Rīgas ūdens (Cabinet Regulation No. 174, paragraph 19). They can be requested by the developer or one of the co-owners, and for a two-storey apartment house also by the house manager. Rīgas ūdens prepares them within 11 working days free of charge.

Because of the high groundwater, it is worth planning outside excavation here for a drier period. Inside the house a single sewer outlet has to be prepared at the right height, and that is work we can do while the design is being prepared.

Technical conditions for a new connection: Cabinet Regulation No. 174, the Rīgas ūdens page on properties without a connection. Checked 05.11.2026.

If the house has a holding tank or septic tank

Riga Binding Regulation No. 66 states that waste water from decentralised systems may only be removed by septic haulers listed in the register of the Housing and Environment Department. The hauler gives the owner a supporting document with the address, volume and date, so these documents are worth keeping.

In Aplokciems a holding tank has one extra risk: if its walls or the inlet joint are not watertight, the high groundwater seeps in and the tank fills faster than from the house drains alone. If the tank regularly fills faster than before, check the joints. On the house side, drain cleaning costs from €40.

Septic hauler register and supporting document: Riga Binding Regulation No. 66 (likumi.lv). Checked 05.11.2026.

Winter in wet ground: inlets and outside taps

Where groundwater is high, inlet pipes are often laid shallower so that they are not in water. In winter this means less frost margin, especially where the pipe comes out of the ground and enters the house through an unheated basement or veranda.

Before the frosts, shut off and drain the outside tap and the watering line, and check that the inlet section by the foundations is insulated. If the water has stopped running anyway, thawing frozen pipes costs from €55; do not start thawing with an open flame.
