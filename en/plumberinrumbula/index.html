Plumber in Rumbula, Riga

A plumber in Rumbula usually works not in apartments but at businesses and on family allotments. There is practically no housing in the district, the land is used by car sales and repair businesses, and utility provision is poor. That decides which plumbing jobs matter here.

Businesses, allotments and weak utilities

There is practically no housing on Rumbula's 697.8 ha. Public descriptions mention car sales and repair businesses and family allotments, as well as poor utility provision.

In a business building the plumbing is usually not complicated, but it is used heavily: a staff toilet, sinks, a water heater (boiler) and a washing area. If any of these breaks down, work stops too, so these repairs can rarely wait.

Before sending a request, it is worth finding out where the building gets its water from and where its waste water goes. In Rumbula this cannot be taken for granted, and the answer decides whether the job is on the pump, on the building's internal network or on the pipe to the tank.

Car workshop premises: floor drains, sand and sinks

In car repair premises, sand, mud and dirt from hands and parts end up in the floor drains and sinks. The sand settles in the trap and the horizontal pipe, and the flow gradually drops until the water no longer drains away.

  • It is worth cleaning floor drains and traps regularly, before they block completely.
  • If the outlet blocks often, check the pipe's fall and whether the floor drain has a sand trap.
  • Drain cleaning costs from €40; the price depends on the length of the outlet and how firmly the deposits have compacted.

Family allotments: seasonal water and frost

On allotments water is usually used only in the warm season, and the pipes run shallow or along the surface. If the system is not drained before the frost, the water in the pipes, taps and pump freezes and can split them.

  • In autumn, shut off the supply, open the taps and drain the water from the pipes and the pump.
  • In spring, check the joints before turning the water on; a crack is often noticed only once water is already leaking.
  • If the well pump does not work in spring, well pump repair costs from €80.

Local sewage systems and a future connection

In 2025 Rīgas ūdens started designing a shared water and sewerage system for Dārziņi and neighbouring districts, and Rumbula businesses will be able to connect to it. When and in which streets this will happen has not yet been set publicly.

Until then, sites without a connection use local systems. Riga Binding Regulation No. 66 states that waste water from them may only be removed by septic hauliers listed in the department's register of septic hauliers, and the haulier issues a document with the address, volume and date. These documents are worth keeping.

Rīgas ūdens is responsible only up to the building's metering chamber or the building's outer wall. If a pipe bursts or the sewer blocks inside the building, the repair is the building owner's responsibility, while the tenant's share is set by the lease.

Water heaters and hot water in small industrial buildings

In workshops and warehouses, hot water often comes from a small water heater under or above the sink. It has two typical faults: the safety valve starts dripping constantly, or the water heater stops heating.

  • A dripping safety valve can mean the inlet pressure is too high or the valve is worn; the valve must never simply be plugged.
  • If the water heater starts hissing and heats up more slowly, sediment has built up in the tank, and cleaning costs from €35.
  • In an unheated room, the water heater must be drained in winter if the heating is switched off, or the tank may burst.
