Plumber in Brasa, Riga

A plumber in Brasa works in a district whose buildings resemble Riga's historic centre more than a microdistrict: residential buildings here alternate with a hospital, a prison, shops and offices. Brasa is also one of the places where water from two sources meets in the city pipes, and in some blocks groundwater is close to the surface. Both directly affect what breaks in an apartment and how it is repaired.

Mixed development: apartments next to institutions

The districts portal says Brasa is dominated by the mixed and residential areas typical of the historic centre, about 43% of its area, and the district has many public buildings, such as the Maternity Hospital and Brasa Prison.

A mixed-use building often has both apartments and premises with another use, and they share the water supply and sewerage. If a riser has to be shut off or work done in the basement, it also affects a shop or office on the ground floor. So before the work it is worth knowing who in the building is responsible for the shared networks and how to contact them.

The inlet valve in an old building: when the repair is not only yours

The valve at the apartment inlet is not legally only yours. In a judgment of September 2023 (case SKC-535/2023, a claim against Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks), the Senate of the Supreme Court recognised it as part of the whole building's water supply, because it cannot be separated from the common property utility lines.

In Brasa buildings with pipes that have not been replaced for a long time, the inlet valve itself is often the weak spot: it is rusted, no longer closes fully or starts seeping at the spindle. While that is the case, any work beyond it (replacing a mixer tap, a toilet supply, a washing machine connection) can only start after the house manager shuts off the riser.

If the valve leaks, first tell the house manager. Once the water is shut off and the valve works, work in the apartment can continue as usual.

Ownership of the inlet valve: Senate judgment SKC-535/2023, adopted 21.09.2023, published on at.gov.lv. Checked 05.11.2026.

Where Baltezers and Daugava water meet

About half of Riga's drinking water comes from the Baltezers groundwater reservoir, and the other half is treated from Daugava surface water (LSM, 2018). In measurements by RTU scientists in 2021, Brasa was among the places where the two waters mix in the pipes, and there the risk of cloudy water is higher.

In an apartment this is usually noticed not as brown water but as a stream that slowly gets weaker. The fine particles settle in the mixer tap aerator, the shower head and the inlet screen of the dishwasher or washing machine. If only one tap runs weakly, the problem is in that tap, not in the whole building.

Water sources: LSM, 2018. Mixing zone and risk of cloudy water: RTU 2021, LV portāls. Checked 05.11.2026.

Flushing on Cēsu iela and Mēness iela 29.09.2026–02.10.2026

From 29.09.2026 to 02.10.2026, Rīgas ūdens flushed an 8.5 km trunk water main from Cēsu iela to the Dreiliņi roundabout. In Brasa possible cloudiness was indicated on Cēsu iela and Mēness iela. During flushing the water flow strips iron and minerals off the pipe walls, and these particles travel through the building inlet to the apartments.

  • Brownish water in all taps: this is the supply, not your plumbing. Let the cold water run and, if the colour does not clear, tell the house manager.
  • Water clear but running weakly: a clogged aerator or screen. If cleaning does not help, low water pressure diagnosis costs from €25.
  • A tap drips or turns stiffly after the sediment: the cartridge may be damaged; mixer tap repair from €25.
  • The water heater (boiler) crackles when heating and the water takes longer to warm: sediment has built up at the bottom of the tank. Water heater repair starts from €45, cleaning from €35.

Shallow groundwater and damp basements

In part of Brasa groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m, which makes building harder; in the centre around Miera iela conditions are fairly good. For buildings with a basement this means damp in exactly the room that usually holds the building's sewer outlet and water inlet.

In a damp basement steel pipes and brackets rust faster, and a smell in the stairwell often comes from a dried-out floor drain or a loose cleaning access cover. Sewage smell diagnosis and removal starts from €35. If the source of the smell is in the building's shared network in the basement, the house manager orders the work.
