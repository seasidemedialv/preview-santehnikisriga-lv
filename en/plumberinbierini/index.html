Plumber in Bieriņi, Riga

A plumber in Bieriņi almost always works in a detached house: detached-house areas cover more than half the district, and the plan is to keep its garden character. In part of Bieriņi city networks were built up to the plot boundary as early as 2011–2015, but many owners still do not use them. This page explains what has to be done between the boundary and the house.

A district of detached houses and gardens

Detached-house areas cover 57.5%, or 245.7 ha, of Bieriņi, and the plan is to keep the district's character of detached houses and gardens. Plumbing work here is on the scale of the house and yard: the inlet, internal sewerage, the water heater (boiler) and garden water.

A house with a garden has its own typical weak spots. An outside tap and garden water pipe that are not drained before the frost burst in winter. An inlet from the street laid too shallow freezes in severe cold. If the house has its own well or borehole for watering or for the whole house, there is also a pump, a pressure tank and a pressure switch.

Two generations of networks: phases 1–2 and phase 4

Bieriņi was already included in the Rīgas ūdens project “Development of Water Management in Riga, Phases 1–2” (1996–2006), which renewed and extended the water supply and sewer networks with the aim of providing mains services to at least 95% of Riga residents.

Later, in phase 4 (2011–2015), new networks with branches up to each plot boundary were built in another part of Bieriņi; in the project this area was called “Mārupe” and also included part of Pleskodāle. That is why the district has streets with fairly new networks and streets with older ones. Rīgas ūdens states the connection options and location for each property.

Only 9% connected along the phase 4 networks

In 2017 LSM reported that along the networks built in phase 4, only 9% of properties had connected in the Mārupe, Katlakalns and Bolderāja project areas. In 2023 LSM also wrote that around 3,000 households in Riga could connect but had not done so, and that in total almost 10,000 still dealt with their waste water locally.

A branch at the boundary is not yet a connection. Rīgas ūdens provides the service up to the ownership boundary: usually the outer edge of the wall of the metering chamber, or, if there is no chamber, the red line of the street. The section from there to the house is built and maintained by the owner, as is all the plumbing inside the house.

Phase 4 compensation was available if the connection was built by 31.12.2025 and the application submitted by 01.04.2026. Both deadlines have passed, so costs now have to be planned without this support, unless Rīgas ūdens points to another option.

From a septic tank to the city network: work inside the house

  1. 1

    Find all the outlets

    In an old house the kitchen, bathroom and basement floor drains may run to different tanks.

  2. 2

    Join them into one outlet

    The position and height of the outlet must match the design of the outside connection pipe.

  3. 3

    Check the fall of the internal pipes

    After the switch-over all the water will go one way, and weak spots will show quickly.

  4. 4

    Disconnect the old tank

    The septic tank or holding tank is disconnected from the house sewerage so that waste water does not go in two directions.

Groundwater in Bieriņi: mostly deep

In most of Bieriņi groundwater is deeper than 1.5 m, and only in the north-western part is it shallower. On most plots the trench for the connection pipe stays dry, and basements suffer from groundwater less often than in low-lying districts by the Daugava.

In the north-western part, where the water is closer to the surface, the basement may need a sump pump, and the connection pipe needs a stable bed. If there is a borehole or well with a pump in the garden, repairing it costs from €80.

Buying or selling an old detached house

Many houses in Bieriņi change hands, and buyers are often unsure whether the house is connected to city sewerage or still uses a septic tank. It is worth finding out before the deal, because the cost of connecting now has to be paid by the owner.

A pre-sale plumbing inspection costs from €60. It covers the inlet and valves, pipe materials, the water heater, the condition of the drains and where the house sewer outlet goes.
