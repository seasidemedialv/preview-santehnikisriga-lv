The detached-house area from the 1930s

Detached-house areas take up 14.7% of Teika, or 68.9 ha, and to a large extent this is the area laid out in the 1930s around Lielvārdes, Bajāru and Aizkraukles streets. By 1939, 531 residential buildings had been built, 389 of them two-storey, and even then the high groundwater made construction harder.

The plumbing in these houses has been rebuilt many times, often separately on each floor. The first floor may have a bathroom added later with its own pipe that is joined to the old drains on the ground floor. When working in such a house, we first trace where each waste pipe goes and where the building's outlet is.

Sewage that drains into the ground, and damp basements

A public description of Teika says that many houses are not connected to the city's main sewer, water collects in basements, and even in houses connected in Soviet times the sewage in many places drains into the ground. This means the old outlet from the house to the street may be cracked or collapsed.

  • Signs: a smell in the basement, damp foundations in one spot, water draining slowly from all the house's drains at once.
  • If all the drains in the house block and cleaning helps only for a short time, the reason is often a sunken or broken section of the outlet.
  • Water in the basement is not always groundwater: it can also be a leak from the sewer or the inlet.

Sewer smell diagnostics cost from €35, drain cleaning from €40. If the outlet has collapsed, it is rebuilt, and that is a good opportunity to prepare the connection to the new network at the same time.

Description of unconnected houses and sewage draining into the ground: lv.wikipedia.org. Checked 05.11.2026.

The phase 6 networks and the 31.12.2026 deadline

In the Rīgas ūdens project "Water Management Development in Riga, Phase 6" the networks are being extended in Teika, Imanta and Ziepniekkalns, 11.07 km in total: 3.57 km of water main and 7.50 km of sewer. In Teika the work began in 2022.

For Teika owners whose street has the new network, the municipality covers the connection design up to €739 and the construction work at €1,800; for certain groups of residents the construction work is covered in full. The condition: the connection must be completed by 31.12.2026, and the compensation application must be submitted by 01.04.2027. On the day this page was checked, 05.11.2026, less than two months remained until the construction deadline.

The first step is the Rīgas ūdens technical conditions; they are prepared in 11 working days free of charge (Cabinet Regulation No. 174). The inside of the house can be put in order now: one outlet in the right place and at the right height, internal pipes with a fall, and disconnection of the old line.

Where the Rīgas ūdens part ends

Rīgas ūdens provides its service up to the ownership boundary. Most often this is the outer edge of the wall of the metering chamber; if there is no chamber, the boundary is the street's red line, that is, the plot boundary. Everything beyond it, including the old outlet under the yard, is the house owner's responsibility.

In Teika this is especially important, as the old outlets run under gardens and driveways. If a leak or collapse is between the boundary and the house, the owner repairs it and pays for it.

Ownership boundary: Rīgas ūdens, responsibility boundaries for water services. Checked 05.11.2026.

The Šmerļupīte and Lielvārdes Street: where groundwater is higher

Overall, Teika's geological conditions are relatively favourable. In the north-east around the Šmerļupīte stream the groundwater is high, while in the south-west and near Lielvārdes Street conditions are difficult in places.

In these places a sump pump is useful in the basement (repair from €75), and the connection trench may need pumping out. If water stands in the basement in spring, expect the trench to fill too.
