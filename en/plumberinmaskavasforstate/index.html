Plumber in Maskavas forštate, Riga

Our plumber in Maskavas forštate often works in houses built long before water mains and drains were put into them. The district lies on low ground by the Daugava, and the groundwater there is mostly close to the surface. These two facts decide where to start when a pipe leaks or water collects in the basement.

19th-century wooden houses and pipes added later

Officially this is the Latgale district. Most of the surviving buildings are wooden houses from the 19th century, none older than the 18th century, and the street grid is based on the 1813 plan. Water and drainage in such houses were usually put in later and rebuilt piece by piece several times.

So one house often combines steel pipes, cast-iron drains and new plastic sections. Every transition between materials is a possible leak point. Before a repair it is worth knowing where the pipe runs: through the wall, under the wooden floor or through the attic, because that decides how much has to be opened up.

In a wooden house a leak is more dangerous than in a panel building. Water soaks into the beams and the filling between floors, and the damp stays there for a long time. If the floor under the sink or by the toilet turns soft or dark, do not wait: water leak repair costs from €45.

What in an old block of apartments is yours, and what belongs to everyone

Article 3(2) of the Apartment Property Law (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums) states that an apartment includes the utility lines up to the risers, which are common property. Article 4(1) in turn counts the utility systems serving the building as the building's common property. This boundary also applies to an old wooden house divided into apartments.

Article 6(2) of the Residential Property Management Law (Dzīvojamo māju pārvaldīšanas likums) includes among the mandatory management tasks the provision of water supply and drainage, and the inspection, maintenance and routine repair of the utilities. If the shared drain in the basement is blocked or a pipe serving several apartments is leaking, that is the house manager's job.

In historic houses a riser does not always look like a riser: it may be a pipe in a corner that runs through two neighbours' apartments. If it is not clear whether a pipe is common property, ask the house manager before the work, not after it.

Responsibility boundary: Apartment Property Law, Article 3(2) and Article 4(1); house manager's duties: Residential Property Management Law, Article 6(2) (likumi.lv). Checked 05.11.2026.

Low ground by the Daugava: groundwater shallower than 1.5 m

Historically the district flooded every year, so a dyke was built along the Daugava. The groundwater is mostly shallower than 1.5 m, and only 1.5–3 m in the north-west. For the basement and the ground floor this means constant damp, and the sewer outlets run through wet ground.

  • Water in the basement after rain or in spring: most likely groundwater. A pump in a pit that switches on by itself helps; sump pump repair costs from €75.
  • Water in the basement in dry weather, especially warm water: look for a leaking pipe or the heating system.
  • A smell on the ground floor: often a dried-out trap or a damaged old outlet; sewage smell diagnosis from €35.
  • Slow drainage throughout the house: the outlet in the basement or yard may be blocked or have sunk into the wet ground.

Krasta Street, the Southern Bridge and the Island Bridge: flood risk up to 10%

The area between Krasta Street, the Southern Bridge, the Daugava and the Island Bridge faces a flood risk of up to 10%. If your building is in this strip, the plumbing in the basement needs a bigger margin: the pump should be checked before autumn, and the drain cleaning hatches must be accessible.

If the basement has already flooded, first switch off the electricity in that room and only then start pumping. Once the water has receded, check whether any pipes have sagged or shifted, and if you need paperwork for the insurer, flood documentation costs from €70.

Before a repair in an old house: what changes the price

We give an exact price after assessing the situation and agree it before the work. In Maskavas forštate three things matter most: whether the pipes are accessible or the wooden floor or panelling has to be opened, what state the old threads and cast-iron joints are in, and whether the water can be shut off for your apartment alone.

If you plan to renew the whole bathroom, bathroom plumbing starts from €200. Carrying a heavy cast-iron bath out of an old house with narrow stairs is a separate job that is worth discussing in advance.
