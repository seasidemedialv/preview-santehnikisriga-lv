Interwar private houses: plumbing added later

The Jaunciems houses from the 1920s and 1930s were built at a time when running water in a house was not taken for granted. Water and sewerage were put in later, often in several stages, and each new owner added something of their own.

  • The inlet may run shallow or through an unheated veranda, and in winter that is exactly where the pipe freezes first.
  • Drains from the kitchen and from the bathroom may lead to separate pits.
  • Old steel joints rust from the outside in a damp basement.

Before larger jobs it helps to draw where the pipes run in the house. Then you can decide what to repair piece by piece and where it is better value to replace the whole section.

If the house takes water from its own well, the pump and pressure tank usually stand in the basement or plant room. In the damp they rust faster, and the switch contacts oxidise. If the pump switches on too often or the pressure at the tap fluctuates, we start the check with the pressure tank.

City networks in Jaunciems: what cannot be found publicly

In publicly available sources we found no description of which streets in Jaunciems have city water and sewerage. So we do not make claims about any specific address: Rīgas ūdens can tell you for certain, and whether there is a connection chamber at the plot boundary also says a lot.

In 2023 LSM wrote that almost 10,000 households in Riga still dealt with their waste water locally: around 7,000 were waiting for a network closer to home, while around 3,000 could connect but had not done so. Building sewerage costs €600–800 per metre. If your house has a septic tank or holding tank, Riga's rules on local systems apply to it.

Septic tank or holding tank: registration with the Housing and Environment Department

Riga Binding Regulation No. 66 requires the owner of a septic tank, holding tank or local treatment plant to register their system with the Housing and Environment Department. For existing systems the deadline was 31.12.2021, and a new system is registered within a month of being built.

The plumber's work here is the internal house sewerage up to the outlet: blockages, broken pipes, wrong fall and smells. The tank itself is emptied by a septic hauler. If it smells indoors rather than in the yard, the fault is often a dried-out water seal or an unvented pipe; smell diagnosis starts from €35.

Registration obligation and deadlines: Riga Binding Regulation No. 66 on decentralised sewerage (likumi.lv). Checked 05.11.2026.

The shore of Ķīšezers: high groundwater and weak ground

Along the shore of Ķīšezers up to Jaunciema gatve conditions are poor: groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m, the ground is weak and the area waterlogged. Only a shore strip up to 200 m wide floods regularly, while elsewhere groundwater is 1.5–3 m deep.

  • A basement on the shore side needs a sump pit with a pump that switches on before the water reaches the floor; sump pump repair from €75.
  • If the tank fills faster than usual, one reason may be groundwater seeping in through a lid or seams that are not watertight.
  • In weak ground the outside sewer pipe can sink and lose its fall; if a blockage keeps recurring in one place, we check exactly that spot.

1970s apartment buildings by the former paper mill

The Jaunciems apartment buildings were built in the 1970s, and the work in them is different: old risers, apartment branch pipes and standard bathrooms. If the original pipes have not been replaced, they are about 50 years old or more.

In the apartment we can replace the branch pipes from the riser, mixer taps, the toilet and the bath. Riser work is agreed with the house manager, because the water has to be shut off for the whole riser while it is done. Toilet installation starts from €60.
