Plumber in Bolderāja, Riga

A plumber in Bolderāja meets three different kinds of work: houses in the workers' settlement from the 1950s, prefab blocks built since 1965, and private houses that city networks only reached in 2011–2015. Below we describe what to keep in mind in each, and why Bolderāja's water and ground play their own part here.

The silicate brick settlement, the prefab district and the port side

At the start of the 1950s a workers' settlement for the silicate brick factory, with standard apartments, was built in Bolderāja. From 1965 prefab blocks went up, with the district planned for 15,000 residents. The district also has historic low-rise buildings, detached houses and port facilities, but residential buildings take up only 11.4% of its area in total.

In the prefab blocks the issues are the same as in other microdistricts: ageing risers, seepage at branch pipes and blockages. In private houses the main topic is connecting to city sewerage and what happens to pipes in wet ground.

Phase 4 networks: the branch up to the plot boundary is already there

From 2011 to 2015, in the Rīgas ūdens phase 4 project co-financed by the EU Cohesion Fund, new water and sewer pipes were laid in the streets of Bolderāja, and a branch up to the boundary was built for each plot. So the pipe is already in the street, and what remains is the section from the branch to the building and the internal house sewerage.

Cabinet Regulation No. 384 states that if the decentralised system does not meet environmental requirements and city sewerage has been built in the adjacent street, the owner must connect. Everything starts with technical conditions, which Rīgas ūdens issues under paragraph 19 of Cabinet Regulation No. 174. They may be requested by the developer or any of the co-owners; they are free and ready after 11 working days.

Inside the house, all the drains must go to one outlet at the right height, with a fall and no back-falls. The old pipe to the septic tank or holding tank must be disconnected, not left as a second route. We do this part; the outside connection pipe is built according to the design.

On the phase 4 networks: the Rīgas ūdens page on EU co-financed projects. Obligation to connect under Cabinet Regulation No. 384 and the procedure for technical conditions: Rīgas ūdens explanations. Checked 05.11.2026.

Co-financing deadlines in the phase 4 districts have ended

In the phase 4 districts, including Bolderāja, the municipality offered co-financing for connection building work: €1,000 for a connection up to 5 m long, €1,500 for 5–20 m and €2,000 for longer than 20 m. The work had to be finished no later than 31.12.2025, and the municipality accepted applications until 01.04.2026.

On the day this page was checked, 05.11.2026, both dates had already passed. The general Riga co-financing regulations (No. RD-23-207-sn) do not apply to the phase 4 districts, because they had their own rules. If you are only now planning a connection, expect to pay the full cost and ask Rīgas ūdens whether any other support is currently available.

Amounts and dates for the phase 4 districts: Rīgas ūdens news for owners on applying for co-financing. That RD-23-207-sn does not apply to phases 4 and 5 is stated by Rīgas ūdens. Checked 05.11.2026.

The far end of the network: why the water is sometimes cloudy

In RTU measurements from 2021, Bolderāja was named among the more distant districts with a higher risk of cloudy water, together with Daugavgrīva and Vecmīlgrāvis. Water reaches this area through a long network, and deposits in the pipes can be stirred up when consumption changes.

In the home the sediment settles in small places. The aerator in a mixer tap then lets out a thin, spluttering stream, the thermostatic shower valve starts to regulate unevenly, and a layer of sediment forms at the bottom of the water heater (boiler). You can unscrew and rinse the aerator yourself; if that does not help, mixer tap repair costs from €25, and water heater cleaning from €35.

Risk of cloudy water in more distant districts: RTU study 2021, LV portāls. Checked 05.11.2026.

Ground by the Hapaka ditch and the Buļļupe floodplain

In most of Bolderāja groundwater is 0–1.5 m deep, and only in the dune strip is it deeper than 3 m. By the Hapaka ditch conditions are poor, with low load-bearing soils. The Buļļupe floodplain meadow in the north floods regularly, and the ground contains silt interlayers and thin peat layers.

  • In a connection trench water can appear after the first spade's depth, so the outside work is timed for a drier period.
  • In peaty ground a sewer pipe needs a compacted sand bed, otherwise it sinks and dips form where sediment collects.
  • A basement or service pit without a pump with a float switch quickly becomes damp here; sump pump repair from €75.
Groundwater and the Hapaka ditch: apkaimes.lv, Bolderāja geography. The Buļļupe floodplain and soils: lv.wikipedia.org, Bolderāja. Checked 05.11.2026.
