What "Mangaļi" means

Mangaļi is the everyday name for the historical Mangaļi parish and the Mangaļi manor (Mangaļmuiža), and it is sometimes also used for Mangaļsala. There is also Mangaļi railway station on the Zemitāni–Skulte line. The name has no official boundaries.

On the map, the station lies between the blocks of apartments of Mīlgrāvis and Vecmīlgrāvis and the port areas. If your address is officially in Vecmīlgrāvis, Mīlgrāvis or Mangaļsala, the pages for those districts will help too, because there we can describe the facts more precisely.

Blocks of apartments: the riser belongs to everyone

The residential areas of Vecmīlgrāvis are almost entirely built up with multi-storey blocks of apartments, so most plumbing questions in this area start in an apartment. The law counts the risers as the building's common property: the decision to replace them and to pay for it is taken by all the apartment owners together.

The Residential Property Management Law (Dzīvojamo māju pārvaldīšanas likums) obliges the house manager to provide water supply and drainage, and to inspect, maintain and routinely repair the building's utilities. If the riser is leaking or the shared outlet in the basement is blocked, tell the house manager. The supply pipes, taps, toilet and traps after it are your responsibility, and we can repair them.

Common property status of risers: LV portāls explanation of the Apartment Property Law (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), 24.10.2024. What the house manager must do: Residential Property Management Law, Article 6. Checked 05.11.2026.

The end of Riga's northern network and cloudy water

RTU (Riga Technical University) measurements from 2021 list Vecmīlgrāvis among the more remote districts where the risk of cloudy water is higher. This applies to a part of the network, not to any particular building, and it is usually short-lived.

In an apartment it most often shows as a weak stream once the aerator has clogged. You can rinse the strainer yourself. If the tap still runs weakly afterwards or the temperature cannot be adjusted, mixer tap repair costs from €25.

Risk of cloudy water in Vecmīlgrāvis: RTU 2021, LV portāls. Checked 05.11.2026.

High groundwater on the Mīlgrāvis side

Most of the Mīlgrāvis district has difficult building conditions and high groundwater. In a block of apartments this affects the basement, where the shared sewer outlets and the water inlet run.

If there is water in the basement, it is important to tell whether it is groundwater or a leak from the pipes. Either way it is a common property matter to be dealt with through the house manager. At apartment level we deal with leaks and blockages that are your responsibility.

What changes the price of work in an apartment

We give an exact price after assessing the situation and agree it before the work. Access matters most: whether the pipe is exposed or hidden in a tiled box, and whether the damaged spot is in your supply pipe or right at the riser.

  • If the water has to be shut off for the whole riser during the work, the house manager's staff do it, and the house manager charges for it at their own rates, separately from our work.
  • Materials (pipes, fittings, adapters to an old steel or cast-iron section) are charged separately.
  • If paperwork is needed for the insurer after a leak, flood documentation costs from €70.
