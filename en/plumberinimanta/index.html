Prefab microdistricts and detached-house streets: two different kinds of work

The apartment part of Imanta is dominated by five-storey 464 and 467 series and nine-storey 602 series prefab blocks; the district plan of 1967 provided for five microdistricts. Detached-house areas take up about 14% of the district: around Bebru iela, by the Bolderāja railway and between Jūrmalas gatve and the railway.

In a prefab block the first question is usually whether the damage is in the apartment pipes or the building's common property. In a detached house there is no such split: Rīgas ūdens is responsible up to the ownership boundary (usually the outer edge of the wall of the metering chamber, or, if there is no chamber, the red line of the street), and everything beyond it up to the last tap is the owner's responsibility.

464, 467 and 602 series: the building outlasts its pipes

Under Annex 2 of Cabinet Regulation No. 906 the service life set for buildings of these series is 70 years. For cold water pipes and sewer pipes it is 15–30 years depending on the type, and 60 years for sewerage with cast iron fittings. So within one building's lifetime the pipes are expected to be replaced at least once.

If an Imanta building still has its original risers, their standard period has most likely ended, and every new joint on an old pipe is a risk point. So before a bathroom renovation it is worth finding out whether the building is planning riser replacement, so that the wall and floor do not have to be opened twice.

Risers are the building's common property, and their replacement and financing are decided by the owners' meeting. How to prepare such a decision and how the costs are shared is explained in the article who pays for riser replacement. An apartment owner, for their part, can replace their own branch pipes up to the riser.

The Imanta heating plant is in the district. If the building has central heating, a radiator is part of the whole building's system, and its replacement cannot start without first sorting out approval: more in the article on radiator replacement approval.

Service lives: Annex 2 of Cabinet Regulation No. 906 (summary by LV portāls). Risers as common property: Sections 3 and 16 of the Apartment Property Law (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), explanation by LV portāls 24.10.2024. Checked 05.11.2026.

New sewerage in Imanta's detached-house areas (phases 5 and 6)

In the Rīgas ūdens project “Development of Water Management in Riga, Phase 5”, 34.59 km of new sewer network and 18 pumping stations have been built in Berģi, Imanta and Beberbeķi since 2018. The Imanta city networks were put into service in 2023. In phase 6 the networks are being extended further on the Imanta līnijas streets and around Nīcas iela and Vārves iela.

If the network is already in your street but the house still uses a septic tank or holding tank, Cabinet Regulation No. 384 requires you to connect if the local system does not meet environmental requirements. The process starts with technical conditions from Rīgas ūdens: they are prepared within 11 working days free of charge and are valid for one year.

The plumber's part is inside the house. The internal sewer outlet must match the position and height set in the design, the pipes must have the right fall, and the old pipe to the septic tank must be disconnected. If several drains in the house have so far gone to different tanks, they must first be joined into one outlet.

The ground in Imanta is generally favourable, but peat deposits 1–3 m thick are widespread (in built-up areas the peat was replaced with sand during construction), and in the south-eastern part between Slokas iela, Kurzemes prospekts and Jūrmalas gatve the ground is weak. When digging a trench or renewing an inlet, this affects how stable a bed the pipe needs.

Network construction: Rīgas ūdens, EU fund co-financed projects (phases 5 and 6). Obligation to connect: Cabinet Regulation No. 384. Technical conditions: Cabinet Regulation No. 174, Rīgas ūdens. Checked 05.11.2026.

Municipal compensation: the connection must be built by 31.12.2026

In Imanta, as in Berģi, Beberbeķi, Teika and Ziepniekkalns, the municipality compensates connection design up to €739 and building work €1,800, and for certain groups of residents up to 100%. The condition is strict: the connection must be built by 31.12.2026, and the compensation application submitted by 01.04.2027.

The order is: technical conditions, design, building the outside connection pipe, switching over the internal sewerage. If the technical conditions have not yet been requested, start with them, because preparing them alone takes 11 working days. The internal work can be prepared in parallel so that it does not delay the handover.

We help with what is inside the house: rebuilding the sewer outlet, replacing internal pipes and connecting the sanitary fittings to the new outlet. We agree the price before the work.

Compensation amount and deadlines (31.12.2026 for construction, 01.04.2027 for the application): Rīgas ūdens announcement on municipal co-financing of connections in the phase 5 and 6 districts. Checked 05.11.2026. Before starting work, check the current conditions on the Rīgas ūdens website.
